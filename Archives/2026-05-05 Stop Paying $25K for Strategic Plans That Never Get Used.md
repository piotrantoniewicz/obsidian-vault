🚨 New Episode 🚨 Nonprofits are spending $$$ on strategic plans that sit on a shelf and never get executed.  
  
Alexa Diaz Formidoni says you need a strategic plan AND a full fundraising pipeline. They work together, but they're not the same thing.  
A pipeline gives you structure. It's the infrastructure that walks you through every stage of building relationships, from the first touchpoint all the way through retention. It's treating your development work the way a great sales team treats their process, with intention and follow-through.  
  
The full episode with Alexa goes deep on what that pipeline looks like in practice.
