**Nonpartisan nonprofit organizations have a key role to play in helping to ensure free and fair elections.**

**Nonprofits are among the most trusted institutions in American life.** As trusted messengers, nonprofits have a critical role to play in voter engagement and election protection efforts. ### 2026 Is Different

This year many voters, including your supporters, will face obstacles to voting in the 2026 midterms. New districts, new identification requirements and deadlines, purged voter databases, a climate of disinformation and fear, and tremendous political noise all combine into a **real risk that these hurdles will keep eligible Americans from voting** and will silence their voices.

#### The best defense against the erosion of democracy is high voter participation and robust public confidence in the voting process itself.

### The Public Expects Nonprofits to Lead Civic Stewardship

Ensuring the integrity of elections is not a partisan act—it’s an act of civic stewardship and a legitimate charitable activity.

Overwhelming majorities of people across the political spectrum, including nonprofit donors and supporters, agree that [**nonprofits and civic groups should help people vote and speak out when they believe fair elections are threatened**](https://www.mrss.com/lab/new-research-donors-want-to-see-nonprofits-speak-out-for-free-and-fair-elections/)**.**

![](https://www.mrss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/unnamed-1.png)

And it’s effective! In a [Nonprofit VOTE analysis](https://www.nonprofitvote.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/2024-Nonprofit-Power-digital-edition.pdf), **voters engaged by nonprofits saw a 10 percentage-point boost in voter turnout** compared to their counterparts in the same communities.

### What Nonprofits Can Do to Support a Successful Election

Building confidence in elections is not about persuading nonprofit audiences to support a particular candidate or policy. **By proactively educating audiences and supporters, nonprofits help create more informed electors and reduce confusion during election season.**

Nonpartisan nonprofit organizations can weave the following pillars of engagement into existing outreach:

1. Help people check their voter registration, understand what’s on the ballot, and make a plan to vote.
2. Support effectively run elections.
3. Educate about voters’ rights, how to report concerns, and how election disputes are legally resolved.

Inside you’ll also find guidance on how to talk about voting and elections, tips on planning your election protection effort, and what to expect on Election Day and after, during the vote-counting and vote-certification period.

**All of the guidance in this** [**guide**](https://www.nonprofitvote.org/nonprofit-guide-to-supporting-free-and-fair-elections/) **is C3 compliant and completely nonpartisan.**
