## SUMMARY

On this episode of *Create for Change*, we’re joined by [Max Levy](https://www.maxlevystrategies.com/home/#about), a digital and political strategist with over a decade of experience in the nonprofit, government, and campaign space.

Max has played key roles in digital fundraising for the Stacey Abrams campaign and public affairs at Democracy Forward. He continues his impact work today as an independent consultant helping changemakers break through online.

Max walks us through his career journey, discusses what makes a winning digital strategy, and talks about the lessons he’s learned from his experience about what works and what doesn’t in an ever-changing digital landscape.

## HIGHLIGHTS

- **EXPERIMENTATION ONLINE:** Max talks about the importance of experimenting, and shares some lessons learned about stunt marketing through his work facilitating the [viral livestream of President Trump’s name being taken down from the Kennedy Center](https://washingtonian.com/2026/06/10/live-stream-trump-name-kennedy-center/). To Max, experimenting means knowing when an idea warrants high production and investment, and when it requires speed over presentation. Not every situation calls for the most perfect piece of content, but it does have to be intentional and build community among your target audience.
- **DIGITAL FUNDRAISING:** Max addresses a common misconception that folks get wrong about digital fundraising: you aren’t going to raise the majority of your money on social media. This makes it crucial to create consistent places and structures that let patrons contribute to your cause, understand your theory of change, and how their donations make a genuine impact. Max is still a big proponent of email outreach, and emphasizes the importance of a trustworthy brand presence that doesn’t cause audiences to tune you out in the long term.
- **ADVICE FOR 2026 MIDTERMS:** Max provides some insight into tactics that could be useful to digital communicators going into the upcoming midterm elections. Investing in organic social, good quality technical equipment, and paid clipping are some tried-and-true strategies that could get messages that folks *want* to listen to, reaching them.
