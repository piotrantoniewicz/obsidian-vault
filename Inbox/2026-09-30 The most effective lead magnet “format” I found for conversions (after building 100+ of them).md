Over the past 4-5 years, I’ve built 100+ lead magnets.

One of my biggest lessons:

Every business needs 2 "opposing" types of lead magnets:

- **A Pillar Lead Magnet**
- **One-Off Lead Magnets**

So in this piece, I am going to walk you through:

- **The difference between these two** (and why building only one of them can cost you money)
- **Why you need both**, and the simplest way to think about the relationship between them (the engine and the fuel)
- **The most effective format you can use** for your pillar lead magnet, plus the 3 reasons it works so well

And more!

Let’s dive in, shall we?

### The 2 Types Of Lead Magnets Every Business Needs

Let’s start by defining each “type.”

**Your Pillar Lead Magnet** is a polished, usually very comprehensive asset that's strategically designed to drive conversions of your main offer.

These can take weeks, sometimes months, to put together.

Mine is a mini-course called Lead Magnet Ideation Mastery, which lives at [leadmagnetprompts.com](http://leadmagnetprompts.com/).

It's a long-form guide with 7 AI prompts/Claude Skills to help you brainstorm lead magnet ideas. And it's the asset I keep pinned in my profile’s featured section at all times.

**Now, a One-Off Lead Magnet, on the other hand,** is more casual, more timely, and usually smaller in scope.

It also doesn't have to be perfectly aligned with your paid offer, because it's more timely and less timeless.

For example, one of my recent one-offs is [The $10K Infoproduct Accelerator,](https://honored-shallot-9e2.notion.site/10k-Infoproduct-Accelerator-7cabdf10547283209aa101c2f74de3e0) a short guide breaking down the 3 lean product formats I recommend for making your first $10k selling digital products.

It's smaller in scope, it took a fraction of the time to put together, and it gave me a completely fresh CTA to point people to for a few weeks.

Now, you might be wondering...

### "Do I Really Need Both?"

Yes, you do.

Here's why.

If every post and every email you publish promotes the same lead magnet, people will eventually tune out.

One-Off Lead Magnets give you variety. Fresh hooks, new angles, different entry points into your world.

Meanwhile, your Pillar Lead Magnet is doing the heavy lifting in the background:

- Educating new subscribers
- Building trust on autopilot
- And warming people up to buy

Here's an easy way to think about it:

- Your Pillar Lead Magnet is the engine.
- Your One-Off Lead Magnets are the fuel that keeps feeding it new people.

You need both to keep the machine running without fatiguing your audience with the same CTA over and over.

What most people get wrong:

They spend all their time creating One-Off Lead Magnets (checklists, templates, swipe files) but never invest in building a real pillar asset.

That's a mistake.

Because without a Pillar Lead Magnet, you're generating leads without having a system to convert them.

So if you haven't built one yet, that’s the best place to start.

### The Best Format For A Pillar Lead Magnet

In my experience, there’s one specific format that beats everything else when it comes to pillar assets:

**Email courses.**

An email course is an evergreen marketing asset that educates readers on a topic, or helps them solve a specific problem, through a series of consecutive email-based lessons.

Think of it as a very intentional, high-value email sequence.

Here's why they work so well:

- **They're digestible by default.** Unlike a 50-page PDF or a 5-hour video course, an email course drips the content out over time. Nobody has to block off 3 hours to consume it. They just read their emails.
- **They meet people where they already are.** Your email course gets consumed in the place your reader checks every single day. Not a course platform they have to log into. Not a PDF sitting in their downloads folder.
- **They train your readers to open your emails.** This is the sneaky long-term benefit. When someone goes through a 5-day email course from you, they're building a habit of opening your emails. That pays dividends for months, sometimes years.

So if you're going to invest serious time into a pillar lead magnet, I’d highly recommend making it an email course.

### Two Ways To Build One

Now, the hardest part of building an email course is figuring out what it should actually be.

- *What's the value prop?*
- *What's the promise?*
- *What do the 5 days cover?*

Based on my experience, there are two ways to answer that.

### Approach 1: Unbundling

This is when you take your paid offer and unbundle ONE specific module or component, usually something foundational.

You give a compressed but complete version of that one piece through a 5-day email course.

That creates a natural curiosity gap. Once they learn and implement the first piece, they realize they need the other pieces too.

Say your paid offer is a full self-paced course on creating money-making lead magnets. A great section to unbundle would be one of the early ones on strategy and ideation.

Because once people have brainstormed a great lead magnet idea (which is a real win), they'll want help with the rest of the steps.

### Approach 2: The Buffet

This is when you give a high-level preview of EVERYTHING covered in the paid offer.

Each email touches on a different pillar or module at a surface level, building awareness of the full scope and creating desire to dig deeper into all of it.

Same example: instead of a crash course on step 1, you'd give the reader a full but high-level overview of every step and framework you teach inside the course.

The key distinction:

- **Unbundling goes DEEP on ONE narrow problem.**
- **The Buffet goes WIDE across ALL the pieces.**

### How To Pick Yours

So which one should you use?

Unbundling works best when 3 things are true:

1. The module you're unbundling is a gateway concept everyone can relate to
2. It has 3-5 subtopics that map cleanly to 5 emails
3. Completing it naturally leads to wanting whatever comes next

The Buffet works best when your offer has distinct pillars that each stand alone as valuable teasers.

But whichever one you choose, the thing you're really optimizing for is congruence.

The free content has to line up naturally with what you're selling at the end.

Otherwise you've just built another One-Off asset.

### Wrapping Up

Most people are stuck on the fuel and never build the engine.

They keep shipping one-off checklists and templates, which is great for growing your email list, but there's nothing on the other end doing the selling.

So if you only do one thing this week, use the framework above to decide what to make your 5-day email course about, and start there.
