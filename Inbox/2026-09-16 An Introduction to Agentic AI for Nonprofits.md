Every nonprofit leader is hearing about agentic AI, or systems that don't just answer questions but take action on your behalf. It's exciting, and for most small and midsize nonprofits, it's also a little abstract. What do you actually do about it on Monday morning?  
  
In this free webinar, hosted by Nonprofit Tech for Good and presented by TechSoup, we'll make the case that the smartest first move isn't chasing agents; it's setting up well-configured AI assistants. Assistants are practical, low-risk, and available today. They're also the training ground for everything agentic AI is about to ask of your organization: clean data, clear guardrails, and a communications strategy that protects donor trust.  
  
You'll leave with:  
  
\- A plain-English explanation of what agentic AI is, how it differs from assistants and chatbots, and why the distinction matters  
\- Concrete examples of AI assistants nonprofits can set up now — for communications, fundraising, and operations  
\- A readiness checklist for the shift from assistants to agents: data, systems, governance, and the donor-trust questions to answer first  
  
This webinar is sponsored by CharityEngine who will join briefly to spotlight CharityEngine Copilot — the first voice-driven agentic AI fundraising platform.
