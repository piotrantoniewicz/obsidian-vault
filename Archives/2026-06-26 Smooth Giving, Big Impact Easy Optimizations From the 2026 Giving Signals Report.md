## Transforming your donation page into a destination

A donor has found your organization. They care about the cause. They've clicked through to give. And then something stops them.

According to the report, **79% of donors** say unexpected processing fees might make them reconsider hitting that final button. Another **70%** say the same about surprise platform tip prompts. This isn't a generational divide—it's a giving-experience problem that affects everyone

The good news? This is an incredibly easy fix. While **44% of nonprofits** currently feature a platform tip prompt at checkout, removing that extra hurdle takes just minutes.

- **Be transparent early:** Frame your processing fees clearly up front. Donors don't object to the cost, they object to the surprise.
- **Remove the second ask:** Tip prompts pop up right when a donor has already decided to give. Turning them off or defaulting them to zero keeps the focus entirely on your mission.

By clearing these tiny hurdles, you can transform your donation form from a transaction into a celebration of their generosity.

**Tip:** Take five minutes this week to test your giving form from your phone. If it feels like a breeze to you, it’ll feel like pure joy to them.

## Demystifying DAFs for smart, joyful giving

Organizations are thinking big about Donor-Advised Funds (DAFs)—in fact, **64% of nonprofits** are prioritizing donor-advised funds or other tax-advantaged giving this year. But there’s a small disconnect: nearly a third of donors (**29%**) aren't quite sure how DAFs work or if they want to use one.

That’s not a lack of generosity. It's just an education gap waiting for an empowering organization like yours to bridge it.

DAF donors are deeply committed, serious givers. They've already set aside money for charitable purposes. They're thinking about impact with intention. In fact, **42% of Millennials, the generation most likely to increase their giving this year,** have already embraced tax-advantaged giving options. They’re ready to fund your vision—they just need you to show them how simple it can be.

You don't need a degree in tax law to be DAF-ready. Just focus on three practical steps:

1. **Make it visible:** Place your EIN, legal organization name, and a plain-language explanation of how to make a DAF grant right on your website.
2. **Bring it up early:** Talk about DAFs naturally throughout the year, not just during the crazy year-end rush.
3. **Equip your team:** Ensure your frontline fundraisers can explain [what a DAF](https://bloomerang.com/blog/activate-daf-dollars-a-strategic-opportunity-for-nonprofits) is in a few simple sentences and share why it benefits both the donor and the mission.

## Meeting donors where the heart is

Are your messages landing where your supporters are actually looking? The data reveals a simple alignment opportunity— **69% of donors** across *every* generation say email is their absolute favorite way to hear from the causes they love. And yet nonprofits report that social media leads their outreach strategy.

While social media is fantastic for building community, it shouldn't overshadow the direct line to your donors' hearts: their inbox. Underinvesting in email means your most passionate advocates might miss your most inspiring stories.

The secret strategy? **Let email anchor your campaigns.** Once your email strategy is strong, weave in social media, direct mail, and text messaging as supporting acts tailored to different segments. When your communication channel matches their preference, connection sparks effortlessly.
