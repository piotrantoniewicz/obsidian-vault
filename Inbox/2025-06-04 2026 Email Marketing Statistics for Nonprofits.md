A supplemental post to [101 Digital Marketing & Fundraising Best Practices for Nonprofits](https://www.nptechforgood.com/101-best-practices/), the email marketing statistics listed below can guide your nonprofit in launching and maintaining an email marketing strategy that engages supporters and inspires donors.

---

## Email Strategy

- 83% of nonprofits utilize email marketing. Of those, 46% send newsletters monthly, 21% quarterly, 10% weekly, 10% twice monthly, 8% every two months, 3% twice weekly, and 2% send daily. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2026/06/09/announcing-the-2026-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- Nonprofits sent an average of 50 email messages per subscriber in 2025, with fundraising comprising 31 email messages. \[[M+R Benchmarks](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- 63% of nonprofits use personalization in their email marketing. \[[2023 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2023/02/01/announcing-the-2023-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- 67% use segmented email lists. \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
- 34% use UTM codes to track email performance. \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
- Emails with personalized subject lines are 26% more likely to be opened. \[[Campaign Monitor](https://www.campaignmonitor.com/resources/glossary/personalization/)\]
- Personalized calls to action convert 202% better than default calls to action. \[[HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/personalized-calls-to-action-convert-better-data)\]
- 31% use email subscribe popups on their website. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2026/06/09/announcing-the-2026-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- Only 13% of nonprofits are using gated content to grow their email list. \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
- 81% of marketers use generative AI for email creation. Of those, 85% report that AI improved content quality. \[[HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/state-of-generative-ai?hubs_content=blog.hubspot.com/marketing/email-marketing-stats&hubs_content-cta=HubSpot%27s%20State%20of%20Generative%20AI)\]

## Email Engagement

- Nonprofits have an average email list size of 4,191 contacts (547 for small nonprofits and 6,602 for large nonprofits). \[[Neon One Nonprofit Email Report](https://neonone.com/resources/nonprofit-email-report/)\]
- Nonprofits have an average open rate of 28.59% and an average click rate of 3.29%. \[[Neon One Nonprofit Email Report](https://neonone.com/resources/nonprofit-email-report/)\]
- The average open rate for welcome emails is 80%. \[[Mailmodo](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/welcome-email-statistics/)\]
- In 2025, 12% of subscribers unsubscribed and 4% became non-deliverable due to bouncing. \[[M+R Benchmarks](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- Email list sizes increased by 5% in 2025, compared to 4% growth in the previous in 2024. \[[M+R Benchmarks](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- Only 34% of nonprofits delete unengaged subscribers on a regular basis. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2026/06/09/announcing-the-2026-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- 7% stop emailing unengaged subscribers within the first six months, and 22% stop within the first year. 28% say they don’t stop emailing unengaged subscribers until a year or more has passed. \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
- 14% run re-engagement campaigns designed to win back unengaged subscribers. \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
- 94% of nonprofits that utilize email marketing use an email marketing service, such as MailerLite or Constant Contact. The remaining 6% send their email campaigns using BCC. \[[2023 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2023/02/01/announcing-the-2023-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]

## Email Fundraising

- 33% of donors say that email is the tool that most inspires them to give (29% social media, website 17%, print 9%). \[[Online Donor Feedback Survey](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2025/02/15/the-communication-giving-preferences-of-641-online-donors/)\]
- Of the 83% of nonprofits that utilize email marketing, 73% send email fundraising appeals and of those 59% send quarterly, 15% monthly, 15% every two months, 6% twice monthly, 4% weekly, and 1% daily. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2026/06/09/announcing-the-2026-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- 48% of donors cite email as their preferred method of hearing updates and appeals from the organization. Direct mail was the second most preferred channel at 21%, followed by social media (17%), text messaging (8%), and phone calls (2%). \[[Neon One Nonprofit Email Report](https://neonone.com/resources/nonprofit-email-report/)\]
- The average nonprofit raises $1.11 per email contact – $6.15 for small nonprofits, $0.88 for large nonprofits. \[[Neon One Nonprofit Email Report](https://neonone.com/resources/nonprofit-email-report/)\]
- 35% of those nonprofits that send email fundraising appeals raised the amount of money that they expected, 34% raised more, and 31% raised less than they expected. \[[2023 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2023/02/01/announcing-the-2023-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- Email messaging resulted in 16% of all online revenue in 2025 — up from 11% in 2024. \[[M+R Benchmarks](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- For every 1,000 fundraising messages sent, nonprofits raised $54 in 2025. \[[M+R Benchmarks](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- Donor retention increases by 29% for offline donors when you have their email address. \[[NextAfter](https://www.nextafter.com/blog/complete-guide-email-acquisition/)\]
- Fundraising emails with urgency language have a 48% higher click-through rate. \[[M+R](https://www.mrss.com/lab/what-ai-saw-in-3-8-billion-nonprofit-emails/)\]
- Fundraising emails sent from a person have a 32% lower response rate than emails sent from an organization. \[[M+R](https://www.mrss.com/lab/what-ai-saw-in-3-8-billion-nonprofit-emails/)\]
- Fundraising emails without a personal story have a 29% lower response rate. \[[M+R](https://www.mrss.com/lab/what-ai-saw-in-3-8-billion-nonprofit-emails/)\]

## Email Design

- 53% of email is opened on a mobile device and if an email is not designed for mobile, it’s likely to be deleted in under three seconds and as many as 15% of users will unsubscribe. \[[Campaign Monitor](https://www.campaignmonitor.com/blog/email-marketing/7-stats-that-will-make-you-rethink-mobile-email/)\]
- 35% of nonprofits use emojis in email, 18% animated GIFs, 12% polls, and 6% countdown timers. \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
- Including videos in emails can boost click-through rates by 65%. \[[Campaign Monitor](https://www.campaignmonitor.com/resources/knowledge-base/what-is-the-average-ctr-from-videos/)\]
- Emails with graphics have a 1.12% higher click-through rate than emails without any. \[[Unlayer](https://unlayer.com/blog/email-graphics)\]
- Countdown timers in emails can boost registrations by 30%. \[[HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/interactive-email?_ga=2.103694019.21785958.1639976361-1824751501.1619072512)\]
- Subject lines comprising 6 to 10 words achieve the highest open rate at 21%. \[[MailModo\]](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-subject-line-statistics/)
- Email subject lines with questions have a 50% higher open rate and subject lines with numbers have a 17% higher open rate. \[[MailModo\]](https://www.mailmodo.com/guides/email-subject-line-statistics/)
- 35% of nonprofits use emojis in subject lines. \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
