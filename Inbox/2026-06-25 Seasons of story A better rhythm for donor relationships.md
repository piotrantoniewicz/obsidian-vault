Storytelling isn’t just about what you say—it’s about when you say it. Givebutter teamed up with storytelling expert Max Kringen to introduce the *Seasons of Story* framework, showing nonprofits how to match their messaging to each stage of the donor journey, build trust year-round, and create a communication rhythm that strengthens donor relationships over time.

[Check the slides ->](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/e/2PACX-1vRpCV5QEUbFKE3Ky2hGM0Wxw27ItD-oAAURPIixMTwYu4VpHpQ07URlTUkNrx4cVDFOJaj-pcnsIKYx/pub?start=false&loop=false&delayms=3000)

### Key takeaways from the session 💡

**Every season has a different purpose 🌱** Introduce, educate, engage, and remind—each season moves donors one step closer.

**Lead with stories before you lead with asks 📖** Strong donor relationships begin long before a fundraising campaign.

**Not every message needs a donation button 💛** Some of your most impactful communications simply help supporters feel connected.

**Create a rhythm your team can actually sustain 🔄** Consistency beats complexity. Build a cadence your team can repeat year after year.
