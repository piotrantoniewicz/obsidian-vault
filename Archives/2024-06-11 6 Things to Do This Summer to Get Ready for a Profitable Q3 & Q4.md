## They say…

The **best** time to build relationships is when you **don’t need** to be building relationships.

Don’t wait until November and December to start engaging with your donors - it will be too late.

Here are 6 ways to start [warming up your donors](https://www.outintheboons.me/blog/2022/the-wave-method) this summer *before* end-of-year giving season begins.

### 1\. RADICAL GRATITUDE

Calls, notes, emails, text messages - aim for 3 generous “thank yous” this summer.

### 2\. CLEAN YOUR EMAIL LIST

I know this feels tough (you worked so hard for those subscribers!) but keeping inactive subscribers on your list is messing up your data (and your data informed decisions). I recommend putting folks who haven’t opened anyemails in the last 12-18 months through [a re-engagement sequence](https://www.thefundraisingtemplateshop.com/shop/p/email-list-clean-up-swipe-copy) and then removing people who don’t engage.

### 3\. GROW YOUR AUDIENCE

Now that your list is clean, it’s time to rebuild. Grow your email list by inviting people to join your newsletter, inserting a pop-up on your homepage, or putting out an opt-in resource.

### 4\. CIRCLE BACK

Did you tell a story of impact during your last end-of-year campaign? Circle back to your donors and give them an update on that person/animal/place, how they are doing and how your program is helping them.

Want a free email template for this? [Check out the Loop Back email](https://view.flodesk.com/pages/64664d3276364508a70f663c).

### 5\. SPENDING TRANSPARENCY

One of the fastest ways to build trust between your nonprofit and your donors is by being transparent. Try giving your donors [a spending update](https://www.outintheboons.me/blog/2024/6/7/tell-your-donors-how-youve-spent-their-money): salaries, program costs, operation costs & more.

### 6\. SEND A SURVEY

Getting your messaging just right is always tricky. By sending your donors a survey you can uncover what messages are resonating + re-use the language THEY give you in their answers.

***REMEMBER:*** *What you do in June, July and August will impact how much your raise in October, November and December.*
