### CaringBridge

#### Experiment Summary

Timeframe: 04/21/2026 - 04/22/2026

On single post pages, the tribute widget appears right after the post and before the comments section.

For this test, we wanted to test having the tribute widget slide up instead of being the static widget above the comments section to see how it would affect tribute donations.

This was tested on SPP for both mobile and desktop users.

#### Research Question

We believe that adding the tribute widget as a slide up instead of the normal static widget for SPP visitors will achieve an increase in tribute donations because having the tribute widget as a slide up would reduce the confusion of it being assumed as a paywall to interact with the post and encourage users to read the message and donate to CaringBridge.

#### Flux Metrics Affected

The Flux Metrics analyze the three primary metrics that affect revenue (traffic, conversion rate, and average gift). This experiment produced the following results:

0% increase in traffic  
× 50.0% decrease in conversion rate  
× 1.1% decrease in average gift  
50.5% decrease in revenue

#### Key Learnings

The key learnings from this experiment are as follows:

1\. The treatment of adding the tribute widget as a slide up instead of the normal static widget for SPP visitors led to a 50.0% decrease in Donations - Tribute (JEN) with a 95.4% level of confidence. This indicates that the control (the static widget above the comments section) performed better at generating tribute donations.

2\. Revenue - Tribute (JEN) experienced a significant 495% decrease in the treatment group, with a 98% level of confidence. This further supports the finding that the static widget is more effective in encouraging donations.

3\. On a positive note, Comment Left experienced a 129% increase in the treatment group with a 100% level of confidence. This suggests that the slide up tribute widget may have encouraged more engagement in terms of leaving comments on the post.

Based on these results, it is clear that the static tribute widget above the comments section is more effective at generating donations than the slide-up widget. In future experiments, it is important to carefully consider the placement and design of donation prompts to ensure they are optimized for user engagement and conversion. It may be beneficial to test variations of the static widget to further improve donation performance while also considering ways to increase user engagement, such as encouraging comments and social interactions.
