**Why no one is talking about the hacking risks mushrooming across AI models and what we must do about it**

Let’s call a spade, a spade.

While the world races to build faster, smarter, and more autonomous AI systems, very few are stopping to ask: *What happens when these models are exploited, hijacked, or manipulated?*

This isn’t a distant sci-fi threat, it’s already unfolding!

In 2023, researchers at Robust Intelligence found that **major foundation models could be easily tricked** by just slightly tweaking input data—sometimes causing models to misclassify harmful content as safe. The same year, Anthropic’s Claude and OpenAI’s GPT-4 faced proof-of-concept *jailbreaking attacks* through adversarial prompting.

According to a Stanford study on **AI Red Teaming**, *“Almost all large language models deployed today are vulnerable to prompt injection, data poisoning, and malicious fine-tuning.”*

So why aren’t we talking about it enough?

Because model security has become the blind spot of innovation.

We’re funding acceleration, not reflection.

We’re building open weights without open guardrails.

And we’re training models with proprietary data that may already carry hidden exploits.

### The Risks Are Real—and Personal

Imagine:

- An AI model silently trained on poisoned datasets recommending biased hiring decisions.
- A healthcare LLM subtly manipulated to suggest unsafe dosage patterns.
- A defence analytics tool hallucinating location data due to a cleverly planted backdoor.

We’re not exaggerating.

In 2024, a group of researchers from ETH Zurich and Google Brain demonstrated *“model inversion”* attacks that could reconstruct private training data—including faces and medical records—from AI outputs.

So as we scale, we must secure.

Not later. Now.

---

### So, what can be done?

Here are a few grounded steps we’ve begun prioritising at SidLabs—and we hope others join in:

**1\. Fine-tuning doesn’t equal safety.**

Every model we work with now undergoes **external prompt red teaming** and **zero-trust evaluations** before deployment. We treat every LLM, no matter how big, as hackable until proven otherwise.

**2\. Move from “explainable” to “auditable” AI.**

Transparency is not just about the model’s output—but also about its origin, weights, and training trace. We’re backing tools that offer **lineage traceability**.

**3\. Edge AI > API-only dependence.**

Decentralised inference reduces risk exposure. We’re exploring micro-inference containers that **don’t need to ping a central server** —a strategy that matters for defence, legal and mental health apps.

**4\. Human firewalls matter.**

Training teams in **prompt security**, **model governance**, and **adversarial thinking** is part of every project at SidLabs now. The real red team is your human team.
