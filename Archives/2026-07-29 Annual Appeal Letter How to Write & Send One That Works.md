## What is an annual appeal?

**An annual appeal is a nonprofit's yearly fundraising campaign that solicits donations to support ongoing operations and programs.** An annual appeal is a targeted fundraising effort conducted by a nonprofit organization to raise money for its annual fund. It typically involves sending a compelling annual appeal letter or message to your existing donors and prospective supporters to solicit donations that support your ongoing programs and operational expenses. The purpose of an annual appeal is to build and sustain donor relationships, secure flexible funding, and ensure financial stability throughout the year.

Unlike capital campaign appeals (which fund a specific project) or event-based appeals (which drive event attendance), the annual appeal is an ongoing, relationship-driven ask. It typically involves sending a compelling appeal letter or message to existing donors and prospective supporters, reminding them of your mission, sharing your impact, and inviting them to give again.

Sending an annual appeal letter is one of the most important fundraising initiatives your organization carries out each year. It's your chance to share your successes, celebrate the role your donors have played in your mission, and make a direct, heartfelt fundraising ask. For many nonprofits, the annual appeal generates some of the largest individual donations received in a given year, which is why strategy matters.

## What makes an annual appeal letter successful, according to a fundraising expert

Fundraising guru [Claire Axelrad, J.D., CFRE](https://www.linkedin.com/in/claireaxelrad) gives invaluable insight into what moves a donor to action in an annual appeal letter, and the answer may surprise you:

*Most annual appeals don't fail because of a weak ask.*

*After four decades in fundraising, I've learned donors decide from the very first sentence whether this is something they care about.*

*Too many appeals focus on what the organization does rather than why it matters to the donor.*

*They lead with programs, statistics, accomplishments, and institutional needs. Donors, however, are looking for something else. They're looking for meaning. They're asking: "Why does this matter? Why now? And what does it have to do with me?"*

*Before you write a single word, ask yourself those same questions. If you can't find the emotional heart of the appeal, your donor won't find it either.*

*The appeals that get read—and acted upon—connect with a donor's values, concerns, and sense of identity.*

*Before you worry about the perfect ask amount or call to action, answer the donor's deeper question: "Why should I care?" If that answer isn't clear, compelling, and emotionally resonant, the rest of the appeal won't matter. If it is, the ask becomes the natural next step.*

## How to write an annual appeal letter

Sending a compelling annual appeal letter can go a long way toward boosting [your organization's donor retention rate](https://bloomerang.com/blog/donor-retention), which means you'll be able to count on sustainable support from donors in the years to come.

That's why we put together a template that takes you through the process of crafting an engaging and compelling appeal letter.

Follow these tips to ensure that your letter is as effective as possible:

### Five steps to writing an effective annual appeal letter

#### Step 1: Start With a Compelling Opening

Grab the reader's attention in the first one or two sentences with a powerful story, a surprising statistic, or an emotional hook that is directly connected to your mission. Donors receive many appeals; your opening must make them stop and read.

#### Step 2: Share Your Impact Through Storytelling

Include a specific story about a real person, community, or situation your organization has helped. Concrete, human-centered stories make your impact feel tangible. Research consistently shows that narrative activates deeper engagement in readers than statistics alone—use both, but lead with the story.

#### Step 3: Connect the Donor to the Impact

Show donors exactly how their previous contributions made a difference. Phrases like "Because of your gift last year, we were able to…" acknowledge past support and build the emotional case for giving again. Donors who feel seen and valued are far more likely to renew.

#### Step 4: Make a Clear, Specific Ask

State exactly what you need and what that donation will accomplish. Specific, outcome-tied asks outperform vague ones every time. For example: *"A gift of $50 provides meals for a family for one week"* is more actionable than *"Please donate today."* Offering two or three suggested giving amounts—anchored to concrete outcomes—also simplifies the decision for donors.

#### Step 5: Close With Gratitude and Urgency

Thank donors sincerely for their partnership, reinforce any deadline (year-end tax deadline, matching gift window, etc.), and provide clear, frictionless instructions for how to give. Always include a P.S. line—studies show it is one of the most-read elements in any direct mail letter. Use it to reinforce urgency or call out a matching gift opportunity.

## When is the best time to send an annual appeal letter?

**The best time to send an annual appeal is late November through December, coinciding with year-end giving and tax-deductible donation deadlines.**

This timing aligns with the holiday giving season when donors are most motivated to contribute. According to nonprofit industry research, approximately 30% of annual giving occurs in December, with about 10% of all donations made in the final three days of the year. Sending your appeal during this window maximizes visibility and urgency, encouraging donors to make tax-deductible gifts before the calendar year closes.

### Annual appeal letter template outline

1. **Greeting:** Personalized salutation using the donor's name
2. **Opening hook:** Compelling story, statistic, or emotional connection (1-2 sentences)
3. **Impact story:** Specific example of your organization's work and outcomes (1-2 paragraphs)
4. **Donor connection:** Acknowledgment of past support and its impact
5. **Specific ask:** Clear donation amount with tangible outcome described
6. **Closing:** Expression of gratitude, deadline reminder, and call to action
7. **P.S. line:** Reinforce urgency or highlight matching gift opportunity

### Annual appeal letter checklist

Use this checklist to ensure your appeal includes all essential elements:
1. **Personalization:** Donor's name, past giving history, and tailored messaging
2. **Compelling storytelling:** A specific, emotional story illustrating impact
3. **Clear ask:** A specific donation amount tied to a tangible outcome
4. **Strong call to action:** Urgent, action-oriented language with a deadline
5. **Multiple giving options:** Online link, return envelope, and phone number
6. **Expression of gratitude:** Sincere thanks for past and future support
7. **Readability:** Short paragraphs, bullet points, and scannable formatting

Once you've completed this process and feel like you're ready to do a final review of your letter, ask yourself the following questions:

1. **Is your appeal letter easy to read?** If your appeal letter is too wordy or complex, donors may fail to read it all the way through and miss your call to donate. One way to check for readability is to use a resource like the [Flesch-Kincaid grade-level test](https://www.webfx.com/tools/read-able/flesch-kincaid.html) to evaluate what you've written. You should also make sure that your formatting helps with readability. For example, use short paragraphs and bullet points to break up text blocks. This will make it easier for people to scan your letter and quickly digest what they need to know.
2. **Did you write the letter with the donor in mind?** The more donors feel like you value them, the more likely they are to continue supporting your mission. One way to make them feel valued is by using second-person pronouns like "you" more often than you use first-person ones like "I" and "my." You can and should talk about the work you're doing, but you should do it through the lens of how the donors have made that work possible.
3. **Is the appeal compelling?** Your letter should appeal to donors on an emotional level, whether by telling a story about a community member whom they helped with their donations or by sharing what they can help you accomplish by making another donation.
4. **Does your letter include a clear call to action and ways to donate?** Your call to action should compel donors to give as soon as they're done reading the letter, which means you need to create an ask that is urgent and specific. For example, instead of writing "Donate now," you should write something like "Give now and provide shelter to 30 families in need." Make sure you also give your supporters all of the information they'll need in order to make their donations. If you're sending an email, include a link to your online donation page in your message. If you're using direct mail, include a website URL at the bottom of your letter that takes them to your donation page and a pre-addressed return envelope to allow donors to send in a check or cash if they'd prefer to give that way.
5. **Does the appeal express your appreciation for your donors?** Your appeal should [show donors that you appreciate them](https://bloomerang.com/blog/donor-appreciation). Reference the recipient's previous involvement with your organization and let them know what you've accomplished because of their past support. Even if your supporters can't make a donation right away, they should finish reading the letter and feel like they've played an important role in helping you carry out your mission.

## Frequently asked questions: Annual appeals for nonprofits

### What is an annual appeal in nonprofit fundraising?

An annual appeal is a targeted fundraising effort conducted by a nonprofit organization to raise money for its annual fund. It typically involves sending a compelling appeal letter or message to existing donors and prospective supporters to solicit donations that support ongoing programs and operational expenses.

### How is an annual appeal letter different from other fundraising letters?

An annual appeal letter focuses on securing flexible, unrestricted funds for the nonprofit's annual fund—dollars that cover day-to-day operations and programs. Other fundraising letters may target specific campaigns, events, or projects, such as capital campaigns, matching gift drives, or event-based appeals.

### When is the best time to send an annual appeal?

Most nonprofits send their primary annual appeal during the year-end giving season—late November through December—to capitalize on holiday generosity and tax-deductible donation deadlines. Many use GivingTuesday as a campaign launch point, followed by reminder emails in mid-December and a final push before December 31.

### What should be included in a compelling annual appeal letter?

A strong annual appeal letter includes: a personalized salutation, an emotional opening hook, a specific impact story, an acknowledgment of the donor's past support, a clear and specific donation ask tied to a tangible outcome, multiple ways to give, a sincere expression of gratitude, and a P.S. line that reinforces urgency or a matching gift opportunity.

### How can nonprofits create urgency in their fundraising appeals?

Urgency comes from explaining why donors should give now—such as a year-end tax deadline, a matching gift opportunity with a specific expiration date, or an immediate community need. Concrete deadlines motivate supporters to act promptly rather than setting the letter aside.

### What are effective strategies for reaching supporters with annual appeals?

Using multiple channels—direct mail, email, social media, and online donation pages—consistently outperforms single-channel campaigns. Personalizing messages based on donor history and audience segmentation (using a nonprofit CRM) further improves engagement and response rates.

### How can nonprofits measure the success of their annual appeal?

Track key metrics including: total amount raised, donor retention rate, response rate to the appeal, average gift size, and number of new donors acquired. A CRM with robust campaign reporting, like Bloomerang, makes it easy to analyze results and refine future strategies.

### Can recurring donation appeals be part of an annual appeal strategy?

Yes. Including a recurring giving option in your annual appeal encourages donors to sign up for monthly or quarterly gifts, providing nonprofits with a predictable income stream that supports long-term financial sustainability. Recurring giving is also a low-barrier option that appeals to donors who want to give meaningfully but may not be ready for a large one-time gift.

### How important is donor appreciation in annual appeals?

Expressing genuine gratitude is critical. Thanking donors for their past support—and specifically referencing what their previous gift accomplished—builds positive sentiment, increases the likelihood of continued giving, and deepens long-term donor relationships. Personalized thank-you messages that include specific impact updates are especially effective.

The core toolkit includes: a donor CRM or database, an email marketing platform, an online giving platform, a direct mail provider, and social media management software.
