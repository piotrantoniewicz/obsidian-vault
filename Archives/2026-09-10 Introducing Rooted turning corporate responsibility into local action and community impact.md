Businesses are increasingly looking for ways to strengthen trust, engage employees and demonstrate their value to society. While commercial performance remains paramount, there is a growing recognition that long-term success is built not only through products and services, but through meaningful connections with the communities businesses serve.

Many already have community programmes in place. Yet too often this work is undervalued, under-recognised or disconnected from wider business priorities. Important partnerships, local initiatives and employee contributions can go unnoticed, limiting both their social value and their ability to build trust, engagement and reputation.

At the same time, expectations are changing. Policymakers, investors, employees and communities are looking for evidence of real action and local relevance. Corporate responsibility leaders are increasingly asking:

- Who knows about the work we’re leading?
- Are community voices being properly represented in the story?
- How can we share our insights and learning to help others accelerate progress?
- How can we maximise the value of existing programmes without constantly creating new ones?

This is why we have created **Rooted.**

Rooted draws on our experience working with corporates, multinationals, foundations and purpose-driven organisations and helps organisations maximise the impact, visibility and value of their community programmes. Combining strategic communications, stakeholder engagement, partnership expertise and our advocacy approach, we help businesses create meaningful outcomes and credible stories.

We help organisations involve communities in the right way, ensuring lived experience and local voices shape the work itself and the way it is shared. This approach draws on decades of experience in public engagement, advocacy and behaviour change, helping organisations build initiatives that feel authentic, trusted and relevant.

### Our approach

#### Audit

We review existing programmes, partnerships, communications and stakeholder perceptions to understand what’s working, identify gaps and uncover opportunities for greater value, visibility and engagement.

#### Activate

We develop integrated communications strategies that make the most of existing assets and activity. From employee participation and partner engagement to earned media, digital content, leadership communications and first-person storytelling, we help organisations create a joined-up approach that amplifies what already exists.

#### Amplify

We provide ongoing support to deliver the programme, generate content, identify opportunities, capture learning and maintain momentum over time. The result is greater awareness, stronger engagement and more value from existing investments.

Rooted is built on a simple belief that the strongest corporate responsibility programmes are defined by the difference they make in people’s lives, the strength of the partnerships behind them and the stories that bring those experiences to life.

As businesses face growing pressure to demonstrate relevance, resilience and responsibility, community action is becoming a powerful way to build trust, strengthen relationships and show what responsible business looks like in practice.

**Rooted helps organisations turn community action into lasting value for both society and business.**
