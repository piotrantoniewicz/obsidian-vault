## Types of Nonprofit Raffles

Now, not every raffle is created equal. So before you start finding your prizes, it’s helpful to identify what type of raffle is going to help you meet your goal. The core idea stays the same—people buy tickets to win a prize—but the form that your raffle takes can look different.

### The Prize Raffle

This is the most common type of raffle. You get certain items and packages (cue our list of 26 ideas below), and participants buy tickets for a chance to win that specific prize. This works best when you have enticing, high-value prices, as you’ll create more buzz and people will be willing to pay more for each ticket.

### 50/50 Raffle

Instead of a physical prize, the winner gets 50% of the money collected from ticket sales, and your nonprofit keeps the rest. This can be beneficial because there are no up-front costs or work needed from your staff to secure a prize. It’s also fun because the potential prize pool gets larger and larger as more tickets are sold, which can help drive sales.

### Online Raffle

You can do either a prize or 50/50 raffle online. The key is that because everything (ticket sales, payment, drawing) is handled digitally, you can dramatically expand your reach beyond a physical location.

***Bonus idea:*** You could also try a reverse raffle! It’s a fun twist for an in-person event where, instead of the first ticket winning, you keep picking tickets and the last ticket remaining wins the grand prize. It’s a way to build major suspense throughout the night about who is going to win!

## Picking the Right Raffle Prize

If you’ve picked a prize raffle, your next step is deciding *what* your prize is going to be. And, as you might expect, choosing the right prize is going to be key to holding a successful raffle.

It’s easy to assume that the most expensive items are going to be the best prizes, but there are other factors to consider when you’re choosing a prize that is right for your needs.

### Four Factors To Consider

- **Value:** You want your prize to feel high value. It should look and feel expensive, even if you got it donated to you at no cost.
- **Broad Appeal:** Really niche events or prizes (like a ticket to a video game convention, a vegan and gluten-free meal kit, a gift card to a women’s clothing store) can limit the audience of people interested in your prizes and reduce how many tickets you sell. Think of things that nearly everyone can enjoy, like a relaxing getaway, electronics, or cash! You know your audience best, though, so you’ll be able to decide what works for you.
- **Ease of Use:** No one wants to win a prize that requires a ton of setup or a trip that has a load of date restrictions. The easier your prize is for the winner to enjoy, the better!
- **Cost:** Another thing to keep in mind is how to reduce your overhead costs. Think about the businesses, donors, and connections that you have in your community and how they might be able to hook you up with high-quality gifts for free (or at a steep discount). The less you spend on prizes, the more you make.

## Raffle Ideas for Experiences & Adventure

These prizes are all about providing something memorable and exciting instead of just expensive material goods. These “wow factor” prizes draw high ticket prices because they offer a unique story and adventure the winner will never forget.

### 1\. “Staycation” Package

A luxury hotel night, dinner at a top restaurant, and tickets to a local show or museum.

### 2\. VIP Concert Experience

Offer a few VIP tickets and an opportunity to meet local artists (or even bigger ones if you can swing it). If you have a music festival in your area, you could also raffle off a special festival package that includes something like VIP tickets, front row access to the stage, VIP lounge areas, and priority parking.

### 3\. Outdoor Adventure

A guided fishing trip, a hot air balloon ride, or a scenic hike with a high-end gear gift card.

### 4\. Sports Fan’s Dream

Two premium tickets to a major league game, plus team merchandise and maybe a parking pass.

### 5\. Behind-the-Scenes Tour

A unique tour experience at a local vineyard, concert, theatre, or brewery.

## Raffle Ideas for Lifestyle & Services

Next up are prizes that make everyday life easier or add a much-needed touch of luxury and pampering. These are high-appeal prizes because they offer a practical benefit that frees up the winner’s time or reduces their stress.

### 6\. “Me Time” Prizes

A wellness basket that includes gift certificates for a massage, manicure/pedicure, or haircut. You could also throw in a few other wellness goodies like an ice roller, a massage gun, a sleep mask, or a heating pad.

### 7\. Home Refresh

A gift certificate for a professional house cleaning service or a complete organizational consultation.

### 8\. Pet Pampering Package

A year of professional grooming, a massive basket of high-end treats and toys, and a vet gift certificate.

### 9\. The Latest in Tech

A new Apple Watch, a noise-canceling headphone set, or a smart home device bundle.

## Raffle Prizes for Foodies (and Imbibers)

This category is always a hit and is often the easiest to source through partnerships with local businesses. People love to win these types of prizes because they offer a high-quality treat that folks might not buy for themselves.

### 10\. Restaurant Roulette

A bundle of $100 gift cards to different, popular local restaurants.

### 11\. Private Cooking Class

A local chef hosts a small, exclusive class for the winner and their friends.

### 12\. Ultimate Grilling Package

A high-quality portable grill, a meat thermometer, and a local butcher shop gift card.

### 13\. Curated Wine or Beer Collection

A collection of rare or unique bottles that has been curated by a local expert.

### 14\. Monthly Coffee or Tea Subscription

A year-long delivery of specialty brews.

### 15\. Baking Bonanza

A high-end stand mixer, gourmet ingredients, and a cookbook bundle.

## “Go Big or Go Home” Raffle Ideas

These prizes are the heavy hitters that can generate universal excitement and sell a huge volume of tickets due to their high perceived value. Think about the biggest, most broadly appealing items that capture attention immediately.

### 16\. Vacation Getaway

A one-week stay at a condo or timeshare (often donated by a board member or supporter).

### 17\. Big-Screen Entertainment

Get a large, big-screen TV and home-entertainment sound system.

### 18\. Cold, Hard Cash

A $1,000 “Cash Prize.” (Always a huge draw, but make sure to check your local laws!)

### 19\. New Laptop or Tablet

A highly sought-after device, like a MacBook Air or high-end tablet.

### 20\. A Car Lease

A dealership sponsors a one-year lease on a smaller car. For a prize like this, you’ll need a strong, committed partnership.

## Niche & Creative Raffle Ideas

These concepts move beyond the standard gift basket and allow you to tailor your prize directly to a specific audience or need. Creative prizes like this can pay off well if you use them with the right audience.

### 21\. Instant Library

A Kindle or e-reader loaded with gift certificates for books.

### 22\. Themed Basket

Instead of a generic basket, try a Movie Night Basket (projector, popcorn machine, candy) or a Gardener’s Toolkit (tools, seeds, nursery gift card).

### 23\. Parking Spot for a Year

If you’re located in a major city, or really anywhere, where parking is a pain, raffle off a downtown parking spot. This can be a big hit!

### 24\. “Take a Night Off” Basket

Restaurant delivery service gift card, a high-quality blanket, and streaming service subscriptions.

### 25\. Meet-and-Greet

A chance to meet a local celebrity, politician, or influential community member.

### 26\. A One-On-One Lesson

Do you or your team have skills that others might want to learn? You could raffle off training sessions like an art workshop, garden consultation, or music lesson.
