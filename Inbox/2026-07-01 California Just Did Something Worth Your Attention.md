### TEAMS! Newsletter | Special Update | July 1, 22026

Governor Newsom announced a first-of-its-kind partnership that gives every state agency and every city and county discounted access to Claude, along with free workforce training and technical support. Not a pilot. Not a press-release promise. A procurement pathway, now live via the state's new **SITeS portal**. Here's the line that matters most:

> ***"AI should not replace the human work of government; it should help our workers move faster, solve problems more effectively, and deliver better results for Californians."***

This is the right frame for the nonprofit sector, too. Three things nonprofit leaders should take from this:

### The state is legitimizing AI adoption.

When the Governor's office pairs discounted access with mandatory training and civil-rights-focused procurement guardrails, it signals to counties, contracted service networks, and the entire public-serving ecosystem. Expect this posture to shape how state departments provide AI guidance to their nonprofit partners.

### Nonprofits are not automatically included in this deal.

This is limited to state agencies and local governments, not the community-based nonprofits that deliver most direct services under state contract. If your organization operates in that in-between space (state-funded, nonprofit-run), don't assume pricing or access will flow down. Ask your state partners directly.

### The AI conversation has shifted from "should we" to "how, responsibly."

For nonprofits still debating whether AI belongs in mission-driven work, the state of California has just answered that question for its own workforce, with training, guardrails, and a human-in-the-loop framework built in from day one. That's a credible model to borrow from, regardless of your budget or sector.

At AbilityPath, we've been building our own AI governance and literacy work for exactly this moment, because the organizations ready to ask smart questions now will be the ones positioned to benefit as access extends further into the nonprofit ecosystem.

> ***AI won't replace the human work of this sector. Done right, it clears space for more of it.***
