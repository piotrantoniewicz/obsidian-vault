Too often, the issues shaping our future struggle to break beyond their bubble. This summer feels different. Extreme heat is no longer an abstract warning. Children’s online safety is no longer a niche policy debate. Questions about trust, where capital flows and who gets a voice in shaping change are reaching wider audiences and attracting fresh attention. That’s encouraging, but awareness on its own doesn’t change much. The bigger opportunity is what comes next. How do we turn attention into action? How do organisations build trust when certainty feels in short supply? How do communicators help promising ideas reach the people who need them most?

In this edition we explore some of the conversations breaking through and what it takes to turn a moment of interest into lasting change.

## Breaking out of the bubble.

You didn't need to attend London Climate Action Week to know something had shifted. While thousands gathered across the city to discuss climate action, millions more were simply trying to sleep through another unusually hot night. For the first time in a long while, climate conversations seemed to extend beyond the usual audience. Rajshree Sikaria’s [reflections](https://forster.co.uk/climate-is-breaking-out-of-its-bubble-reflections-from-london-climate-action-week/?utm_campaign=Main%20newsletter&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-9z09AE7h1fxMBAkTxUpj1GxBpyfGGKfI92tnc7R4U9qk82nEiCpMkagSwl6iBW__KIzqzd) explore what happens when an issue becomes more immediate and what this shift means for organisations trying to engage wider audiences, build broader coalitions and turn growing awareness into meaningful action.

As audiences become more sceptical of polished corporate messaging, authenticity matters more than ever. Speaking to Reuters, George Ames [shares](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/sustainable-finance-reporting/how-letting-go-script-allows-brands-tell-better-sustainability-stories--ecmii-2026-06-11/?utm_campaign=Main%20newsletter&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-9z09AE7h1fxMBAkTxUpj1GxBpyfGGKfI92tnc7R4U9qk82nEiCpMkagSwl6iBW__KIzqzd) why the strongest sustainability stories often emerge when organisations stop trying to control every word and start communicating with greater honesty, nuance and confidence.
