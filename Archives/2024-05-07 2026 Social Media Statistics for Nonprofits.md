A supplemental post to [101 Digital Marketing & Fundraising Best Practices for Nonprofits](https://www.nptechforgood.com/101-best-practices/), the statistics listed below can guide your nonprofit in creating and maintaining a successful social media strategy.

---

## Facebook

- Facebook has 3.07 billion monthly active users of which 65% access the site daily and spend an average of one hour and 7 minutes per day on the platform. \[[Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/facebook-stats-for-marketers/)\]
- By 2027, Facebook will reach 75% of the world’s population and is currently the world’s third most trafficked website after Google and YouTube. \[[Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/top-websites/)\]
- 61% of its users worldwide are millennials and Gen Z. \[[Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/187549/facebook-distribution-of-users-age-group-usa/)\]
- 93% of nonprofits use Facebook Pages, down from 96% in 2023. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Nonprofits have an average of 53k followers on Facebook. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- On average, organic posts reach 5% of their followers on Facebook. \[[Social Status](https://www.socialstatus.io/insights/social-media-benchmarks/facebook-organic-reach-rate-benchmark/)\]
- Nonprofits post an average of 5.5 times per week to their Facebook Page. \[[Rival IQ Social Media Industry Benchmark Report](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/#title-nonprofits)\]
- Nonprofits have an average engagement rate of 0.046% on Facebook. \[[Rival IQ Social Media Industry Benchmark Report](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/#title-nonprofits)\]
- 53% of nonprofits spend on social media advertising. Of those that spend on social media ads, 98% spend on Facebook. \[[Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2023/02/01/announcing-the-2023-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- Facebook/Meta has an average cost per lead of $3.64 compared to $10.69 on TikTok. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- Facebook/Meta results in an average return on ad spend (ROAS) of $0.76 for fundraising ads compared to $0.04 on TikTok. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- The average cost per donation via Facebook Ads/Meta is $74 compared to $590 on TikTok. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- For every 1,000 email addresses, nonprofits have an average of 1,041 Facebook fans. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- 42% of nonprofits use Facebook Fundraising Tools, up from 37% in 2023. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- 97% of all Facebook fundraising revenue is donated through Facebook Fundraisers. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- The average Facebook Fundraiser has three gifts that average $36 USD — $108 USD in total. \[[M+R Benchmarks](https://mrbenchmarks.com/ "https://mrbenchmarks.com/")\]
- Giving through Facebook Fundraising Tools resulted in 0.2% of all online revenue for nonprofits in 2024 – down from 1.1% in 2023. \[[M+R Benchmarks](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- Facebook fundraisers who are thanked by nonprofits during the fundraising campaign raise 35% more than those who are not. \[[Give Panel](https://givepanel.com/)\]
- 36% of nonprofit professionals say that Facebook helped them most to achieve their communications strategy objectives, followed by Instagram at 26%, and LinkedIn at 25%. 13% say social media does not help them achieve their communications objectives. \[[2026 Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]

## LinkedIn

- LinkedIn has 1.2 billion registered users of which 49% are monthly active users and 16% are daily active users. Monthly users spend an average of 17 minutes per month on LinkedIn. \[[The Social Shepherd](https://thesocialshepherd.com/blog/linkedin-statistics)\]
- 44% of U.S. adults who use LinkedIn earn over $75,000 a year and 51% have a college degree. \[[Sprinklr](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/linkedin-demographics/)\]
- 36% of LinkedIn users are millennials, 27% are Gen X, 26% are Gen Z, and 9% are baby boomers. \[[HootSuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/linkedin-demographics-for-business/)\]
- There are 2.2 million nonprofits on LinkedIn and 23 million nonprofit professionals. \[[LinkedIn](https://nonprofit.linkedin.com/)\]
- 27% of nonprofits worldwide have an official policy to allow staff to work on their LinkedIn Profiles during work hours. \[[Global NGO Technology Report](http://www.nptechforgood.com/global-ngo-technology-report/)\]
- 78% of nonprofits use LinkedIn Pages, up from 49% in 2023. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Nonprofits have an average of 11k followers on LinkedIn. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- On average, posts reach 3% of their followers on LinkedIn. \[[Social Status](https://www.socialstatus.io/insights/social-media-benchmarks/linkedin-organic-reach-rate-benchmark/)\]
- 68% of nonprofits post less than once weekly, 15% post once weekly, 8% post once every other day, 6% post once daily, and 3% post twice or more daily. [\[Global NGO Technology Report](http://www.nptechforgood.com/global-ngo-technology-report/)\]
- Nonprofits have an average engagement rate of 1.5% on LinkedIn. \[[Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-statistics/#linkedin-statistics)\]
- 53% of nonprofits spend on social media advertising. Of those that spend on social media ads, 17% spend on LinkedIn. \[[Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2023/02/01/announcing-the-2023-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- For every 1,000 email addresses, nonprofits have an average of 58 LinkedIn followers. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- 42% of US donors use LinkedIn to research nonprofits to support and 26% discover donation opportunities on LinkedIn. \[[Classy](https://learn.classy.org/rs/673-DCU-558/images/donor-engagement-report-linkedin.pdf)\]

## Instagram

- Instagram has 3 billion monthly active users of which 500 million access the site daily and spend an average of 24 minutes per day on the platform. \[[Sprout Socia](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-stats/#instagram-user-and-usage-statistics) l\]
- 81% of its users worldwide are millennials and Gen Z. \[[Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/187549/facebook-distribution-of-users-age-group-usa/)\]
- 84% of nonprofits worldwide use Instagram, up from 49% in 2023. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Nonprofits have an average of 18k followers on LinkedIn. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- On average, posts reach 8% of their followers on Instagram. \[[Social Status](https://www.socialstatus.io/insights/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-organic-reach-rate-benchmark/)\]
- Nonprofits post an average of 4.9 times per week to their Instagram account. \[[Rival IQ Social Media Industry Benchmark Report](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/#title-nonprofits)\]
- Nonprofits have an average engagement rate of 0.623% on Instagram. \[[Rival IQ Social Media Industry Benchmark Report](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/social-media-industry-benchmark-report/#title-nonprofits)\]
- 53% of nonprofits spend on social media advertising. Of those that spend on social media ads, 47% spend on Instagram. \[[Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2023/02/01/announcing-the-2023-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- For every 1,000 email addresses, nonprofits have an average of 251 Instagram followers. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- 29% of nonprofits use Instagram Fundraising Tools, up from 13% in 2023. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Instagram is the most commonly used platform for nonprofit influencer campaigns. Of those nonprofits that engage in influencer campaigns, 94% work with influencers on Instagram. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]

## Threads

- Threads reached 100 million active users in 4 days – faster than any app in history – 12X times faster than ChatGPT and 55X faster than TikTok. \[[Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/threads-users)\]
- 8% of nonprofits in the U.S. use Threads. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Nonprofits have an average of 12k followers on Threads. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- Threads has 400 million monthly active users and 143 million daily active users. \[[Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/threads-users)\]
- 62% of nonprofits active on X have started building a presence on other emerging platforms. Of those, 88% are experimenting with Threads, 84% with BlueSky, 6% with Mastodon, and 5% with Discord. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]

## Bluesky

- 4% of nonprofits in the U.S. use Bluesky. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Nonprofits have an average of 1k followers on Threads. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- 50% of Bluesky users are in the United States. \[[Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/bluesky-statistics)\]
- 38% of Bluesky users are female and one-third are between the ages of 18 and 24. \[[Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/bluesky-users)\]

- 14% of nonprofits worldwide use TikTok, up from 6% in 2023. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Nonprofits have an average of 3k followers on X. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- 55% of TikTok users are female and 55% are between the ages of 18 to 34. \[[Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/tiktok-users)\]
- 27% of nonprofits worldwide use TikTok and it has an engagement rate of 7.5%. \[[HootSuite](https://hootsuite.widen.net/s/bxdwbgbxtr/socialtrends2025nonprofit_infosheet_en)\]
- For every 1,000 email addresses, nonprofits have an average of 36 TikTok followers. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- 53% of nonprofits spend on social media advertising. Of those that spend on social media ads, 1% spend on TikTok. \[[Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://www.nptechforgood.com/2023/02/01/announcing-the-2023-nonprofit-tech-for-good-report/)\]
- TikTok has an average cost per lead of $10.69 compared to $3.64 for Meta platforms. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
- TikTok results in an average return on ad spend (ROAS) of $0.04 for fundraising ads compared to $0.76 for Meta platforms. \[[M+R Benchmarks Report](https://mrbenchmarks.com/)\]
