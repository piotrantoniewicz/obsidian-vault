## What actually goes into spotting a major gift donor?

Securing a major gift is often treated like a mystery in fundraising, and in a lot of ways, it still is. The ask itself comes down to relationship: trust built over years, a moment read correctly, a conversation that lands right. No algorithm replaces that part, and it shouldn't have to.

The challenge has never been the conversation itself. It’s knowing where to spend your time before you ever pick up the phone. Which donors deserve a closer look? Who may be ready for a larger gift? What do you already know, and what would normally take hours to uncover? That’s where predictive donor insights and Prospect AI, an AI-powered prospect research tool, can make the biggest difference.

These capabilities look at two different sides of major gift prospecting. Predictive donor insights help fundraisers recognize which existing donors may be ready to deepen their giving, based on patterns already present in your CRM. AI-powered prospect research adds broader context about a known donor or prospect, including their capacity, philanthropic history, interests, and connections. Together, they make it easier to identify promising opportunities and enter the relationship-building process better informed.

## Predictive donor insights

Bloomerang surfaces six predictive donor insights that help fundraisers understand what a donor may be ready to do next. Drawing on patterns across CRM data, these insights help teams identify who to prioritize, what type of opportunity to pursue, and how much to ask.

- **Next Gift Likelihood:** how likely a donor is to make a one-time gift in the next three months
- **Mid-Value Readiness:** how likely a donor is to make gifts totaling $500–$4,999 over the next 12 months
- **Major Gift Readiness:** how likely a donor is to make gifts totaling more than $5,000 over the next 12 months
- **Recurring Giving Likelihood:** how likely a one-time donor is to become a recurring donor within the next six months
- **Recurring Donor Churn Risk:** how likely an active recurring donor is to stop giving within the next six months
- **Recommended Ask:** a personalized one-time gift amount based on the donor’s recent giving history

Together, these insights help fundraisers narrow a full donor list into a more focused set of opportunities. But knowing who may be ready is only part of the work. Before reaching out, you still need the context to understand why a donor may be a strong prospect and how to approach the conversation.

## Can development teams identify major donors without predictive intelligence?

The standard approach to identifying upgrade-ready donors involves a combination of gift history analysis, staff memory, and manual research. For your top five or 25 donors, this works reasonably well. For everyone else on a 2,000-person list, it doesn't. If you’re a smaller organization trying to establish strong major gift prospects for the first time, *hours* can be spent on researching who is the best person in your community to ask for a major gift and how much you should ask for.

According to Bloomerang's Fundraising Blind Spot Survey (May 2026), 83% of fundraisers lack a reliable way to diagnose whether their fundraising is performing at its potential. Most organizations have plenty of data. What they don't have is a system that reads it, connects it to external information, and tells them what to do next.

Every hour spent pulling reports, reviewing records, and trying to prioritize outreach is an hour not spent on making the ask and conversations that actually move donors.

ProspectAI adds another layer of context to donor and prospect research. Fundraisers can use it to evaluate new prospects or take a deeper look at existing donors who may be ready for a larger gift. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is predictive donor intelligence?

Predictive donor intelligence uses machine learning to analyze patterns in your donor data and identify what supporters may be likely to do next. It looks across signals such as giving history, demographics, and communication activity to surface insights like who may be ready to give again, increase their giving, become a recurring donor, or need additional stewardship. Rather than asking fundraisers to interpret all of that data manually, predictive donor intelligence turns those patterns into clear priorities they can act on.

### What is Prospect AI in Bloomerang, and what does it include?

Prospect AI is an on-demand research tool inside Bloomerang that generates a structured, source-cited report on a known donor or prospect. It compiles public information about capacity, charitable giving history, professional background, affinity, and connections. It's designed for higher-touch donor opportunities where a fundraiser needs more context when cultivating the relationship or preparing for a major gift conversation

### How does Bloomerang determine that a donor is ready for a major gift ask?

Bloomerang uses predictive models to analyze patterns in giving history, demographics, and engagement activity already in your CRM. Those models are informed by de-identified patterns across the nonprofit sector, while predictions for your donors remain specific to your organization. When a donor shows patterns associated with major gift readiness, they can surface as a high-priority opportunity in Bloomerang. Prospect AI can then add external research context, including capacity and affinity signals.

### Do I need to export donor data from Bloomerang to use donor predictions?

No. You don’t need to manually export donor data or move it into another system to use donor predictions. Bloomerang uses the relevant data already in your CRM to generate predictive insights, which are then surfaced directly in Bloomerang so your team can use them in the same place you already manage donor relationships.

### How often do donor predictions update inside Bloomerang?

**Predictive donor scores** update weekly. That means your team always has current signals when deciding who to call, rather than working from a snapshot that's weeks or months old. A donor who shows new signals of major gift readiness in September will appear in your recommendations that week.

### What's the difference between a monthly conversion candidate and a mid-level upgrade candidate?

A monthly conversion candidate is a one-time or occasional donor whose giving patterns suggest they'd commit to a recurring gift if asked the right way. A mid-level upgrade candidate is already giving at a consistent level but has the capacity and giving signals to meaningfully increase their gift amount. Bloomerang with Dataro scores each separately so your outreach strategy and ask amounts can be tailored to each group.
