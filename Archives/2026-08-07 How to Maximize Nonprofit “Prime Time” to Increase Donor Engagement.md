Summer and early fall tend to be the dead zone in nonprofit communications, but in the last few weeks, **I’ve seen some great examples of organizations making ideal use of this time to *prime* supporters** by [**sharing gratitude**](https://www.the-purpose-collective.com/blog/5-ways-to-make-your-donors-feel-the-love), impact, education, and advocacy opportunities.

**This is what I call “Prime Time,”** *especially* if you run a fundraising campaign in the fall before year-end.

Spend these months celebrating your impact from the first half of the year, sharing any earned media and accolades, educating supporters more deeply on an issue that matters to them, and providing free, easy ways for people to take action (petitions to sign, content to share, questions to answer, etc.).

Let’s take a look at those great examples:

We target HI’s email subscribers, donors, web visitors, and Facebook and Instagram followers with [**videos like this**](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DL0iNUOyGW-/). Here’s an example of our ad copy:

## Using all Channels to Grow & Activate: Sandy Hook Promise

I have no personal connection to this organization, but their omnichannel content this summer has *really* impressed me. They’re running an education + advocacy campaign to ban gun advertisers from targeting children, which I didn’t realize is LEGAL in the United States.

I first saw the campaign via a series of Instagram ads, [**including this one**](https://www.instagram.com/p/DI2uKYUAWZe/?igsh=OGh6b2FzcDNtbXo0). I signed the petition and provided my email and phone number to do so.

I got an automated email and this text message right away:

This campaign is brilliant, because in just a couple months, this organization has:

- Captured my contact information
- Given me an easy action (petition)
- Positioned themselves as subject matter experts
- Strengthened our relationship
- Lit a *fire in my belly* about something that matters to me personally (protecting my son from harmful content)

I will definitely donate to them later in the year when they start asking.

### Final Thoughts

**Aside from a few DONATE buttons in email footers, none of these communications have asked me for money.** This is precisely what makes me *want* to donate to these organizations later.

Over the next couple of months, and again in October/November, I encourage you to use these priming tactics (gratitude, credibility, education, advocacy) to celebrate with your existing fans and impress new ones.
