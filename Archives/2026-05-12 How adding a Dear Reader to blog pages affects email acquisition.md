### Benedictine College

#### Experiment Summary

Timeframe: 03/31/2026 -

Benedictine College has many blog pages on its Media and Culture website that receive significant traffic.

This traffic was already interested in the content Benedictine College offers.

We wanted to test adding a Dear Reader section to capture traffic to blog pages by presenting a free offer at the end of each page.

This was tested on the Media and Culture blog pages and across both mobile and desktop traffic.

#### Research Question

We believe that adding a Dear Reader for blog page visitors will increase email acquisition because the Dear Reader presents a tangible offer to this audience, which is already interested in Benedictine College’s content.

This experiment has a required sample size of 16,350 in order to be valid. Since the experiment had a total sample size of 29,051, and the level of confidence is not above 95% the **experiment results are not valid.**

#### Key Learnings

1\. The addition of a Dear Reader message on the blog pages of the Media and Culture website led to a significant increase in email acquisition.

2\. The treatment group saw a 79.7% increase in Emails Acquired for All Traffic, indicating that the Dear Reader message was effective in capturing visitors' email addresses.

3\. The hypothesis that adding a Dear Reader message would increase email acquisition was supported by the results of the experiment.

Overall, the key learning from this experiment is that the Dear Reader message was effective at increasing email sign-ups on the blog pages of the Media and Culture website. By conducting further testing and iterating on initial results, Benedictine College can continue to optimize its approach to capturing visitor emails and improving website engagement.
