## The Email Engine Overview

Every business in our portfolio — regardless of what it sells — runs the same basic architecture.

1. We drive social traffic to an educational email course opt in
2. That opt in delivers 7 valuable emails to train a subscriber to open them
3. After the initial sequence, we drip them into a core newsletter sending cadence - usually multiple emails per week
4. Their behavior in that core newsletter sequence triggers other sequences aimed at getting the right people to take the right next step

And the two most common problems we see with clients we work with:

- **Their core sending sequence is too light.** They send one email per week, at most. But their subscribers would love to hear from them more often & consume different “types” of emails from them.
- **They have almost none of these advanced sequences set up.** They send the same email to every person on the list and hope people take action. They never take into account someone’s click behavior, open behavior, reply behavior, or time elapsed as a subscriber.

Now, if these sound familiar, you’re in a great spot. Because additional revenue is out there waiting for you to grab it!

## Engine #1: Premium Ghostwriting Blueprint Newsletter

### Key info

- **Business type**: 1:1 coaching program
- Link: [premiumghostwritingblueprint.com](http://premiumghostwritingblueprint.com/)

### Core newsletter sending cadence:

- Daily email, varying between three types
	- **Value email** → pure value, no CTA
		- **Indirect email** → value, with CTA transition
		- **Direct email** → just CTA
- Why daily? That sounds like a lot of emails!
	- Because when your business relies on daily throughput for your sales team, you need your sending sequence to match accordingly. This brings more predictable throughput, rather than hoping a weekly email can fill a 7-day sales calendar

### Three “must have” sequences for this type of business

- **Educational Email Course opt-in**
	- 5-day sequence solving one specific problem or giving an overview of a full system
- **Abandoned Application / Call Remarketing Sequence**
	- For people who apply but don’t end up booking a call, send immediate emails right after, 4 hours after, and 12 hours after like someone abandoning a cart on an e-commerce website
- **Pre-call Nurturing Sequence**
	- When someone books a call, send them a mix of belief breaking, proof building, and offer clarification to answer their main questions before they get on the phone with your team

## Engine #2: Start Writing Online Newsletter

### Key info

- Business type: Digital products + paid community
- Link: [startwritingonline.com](http://startwritingonline.com/)

### Core newsletter sending cadence

- 3x per week email, varying between three types
	- **Value email** → pure value, no CTA
		- **Indirect email** → value, with CTA transition
		- **Direct email** → just CTA
- Due to the higher-margin nature of this business compared to a coaching vertical, we are less dependent on consistent daily volume - which means we’re better off over the long term with a lower but more valuable sending frequency

### Three “must have” sequences for this type of business

1. **Educational Email Course opt in**
	- 5-day sequence solving one specific problem or giving an overview of a full system
		- The opt-in % on our standalone opt in page is 40-50% on average with 100,000+ opt ins to date
2. **Abandoned cart sequence**
	- For any evergreen digital product, we send a handful of additional emails when someone clicks to the product page but does not buy
		- This handles objections, increases certainty, and shows more social proof to increase the likelihood of purchasing
3. **Product launch sequence**
	- 14 day sequence each time we launch a new product or bootcamp
		- 7 days of pure education, CTAing to a waitlist to build hype
		- 7 days of open cart pitching, continuing to educate and add urgency
		- Additional 7 “objection handling” emails to anyone who clicks but does not buy, addressing reasons for not buying + reducing risk by sending more behind the scenes sneak peek content

## Engine #3: Write With AI Newsletter

### Key info

- Business type: Paid newsletter
- Link: [writewithai.substack.com](http://writewithai.substack.com/)

### Core newsletter sending cadence

- 3 emails per week
	- 2 paid educational email
		- Pay wall right above the most interesting part of the email
		- 1 free educational email
		- CTA to upgrade to paid to all free members

### Three “must have” sequences for this type of business

1. **Educational Email Course opt in**
	- Are you seeing a pattern here?
		- Rather than link directly to Substack for this, we link to a free EEC & transfer those subscribers over to Substack after we’ve run the email course. The most common one is [startwritingwithchatgpt.com](http://startwritingwithchatgpt.com/)
2. **New subscriber onboarding sequence**
	- 7-day countdown once someone is moved onto the free email list
		- Shows them around the free newsletter + explains the benefits of upgrading to paid
		- Gives previews of paid newsletters to increase likelihood of upgrading
		- Includes time-sensitive free bonuses if you upgrade to paid during the 7-day window
3. **Periodic upgrade promotional sequences**
	- Every quarter, we pitch paid subs to upgrade to annual (increasing LTV) and free subs to upgrade to paid
		- This is usually done through the creation of an asset that is only available for a limited time for people who upgrade during that window
		- This creates “bumps” in MRR on top of the continuous growth from regular subscriptions

## Engine #5: Ghostbase Internal Newsletter

### Key info

- Business type: Software
- Link: [ghostbase.com](http://ghostbase.com/)

### Core newsletter sending cadence

- 2 emails per week
	- Feature overviews (either new features or driving back to core features)
		- Continuing to educate the user on an ongoing basis
		- Also serves to gather feedback (NPS scores + product requests) as the core CTA

### Three “must have” sequences for this type of business

1. **Free trial promotion onboarding sequence**
	- Once someone makes a free account, we educate them on the product’s free features + contrast them to what they can unlock if they upgrade to paid
		- This is meant to at least get them using the free elements of the product, which increases the likelihood they will want to trial the paid features
2. **Paid user onboarding sequence**
	- After someone opts in for a free trial, we walk them through 5 key activation points that make them more likely to stay on as a paying member
		- The goal of this is to drive them back to the product in case they skipped any steps during onboarding
3. **Win-back sequence for cancelled users**
	- During cancellation, we ask for the main reason they are canceling (price too high, missing features, etc.)
		- We then send congruent emails in the future either 1) hyping up the continued growth in value of the product or 2) showing other people use the product successfully for increased social proof

## Key Email Frameworks Across Our Portfolio

1. **Every vertical gets its own email list. None of them are built on the back of our ‘personal brand’ newsletters.** This allows us to link to these newsletters and opt-ins from our social content in a congruent fashion, rather than to a generic personal newsletter
2. **The more newsletters you have, the more emails you can send.** Each vertical is a separate relationship with a separate audience — someone on your coaching list and your product list can receive both without feeling spammed.
3. **You can’t deposit subscribers in a bank account.** When you send promotional emails, people will unsubscribe. And that’s okay. The value of a dormant email subscriber is precisely zero. If you wanted to maximize your subscriber number, you would never sell anything to anyone. And as a result, you would make zero dollars in revenue.
4. **The highest converting emails show WHAT to do for a big problem, or HOW to solve a small problem within a bigger one.** This is the sweet spot between giving enough value to build trust and leaving enough open to create demand for your paid offer.
5. **Email works for every type of business, yours is not special.** If you sell something on the internet, email should be your highest-ROI channel. If it isn’t, you are leaving money on the table.
6. **Email course lead magnets >>> newsletter opt in pages.** A 5-day email course makes a concrete promise and delivers immediately. A newsletter signup says “get my emails” — but no one wants to get more emails. As a result, we see 40%+ opt in rates on our email course landing pages, versus ~10% on generic newsletter opt in pages.
7. **Send more emails to the people who are interested — they need more information.** Someone who clicked your product page or booked a call isn’t annoyed by follow-ups. They’re trying to make a decision and you’re helping them make it.
