Experiment ID: #204343

### Moody Radio

#### Experiment Summary

Timeframe: 05/27/2026 - 06/30/2026

During Moody Radio’s fiscal year-end campaign, we tested the addition of a countdown clock within the website’s lower-third banner. The objective was to reinforce the campaign’s deadline by providing a highly visible, real-time reminder of the limited time remaining to give.

Rather than adding more copy, the countdown was intended to create a greater sense of urgency in a concise, attention-grabbing way, encouraging more visitors to engage with the appeal and ultimately complete a donation before the campaign ended.

#### Research Question

We believe including a countdown clock in the lower third will increase engagement and downstream donor conversion by creating urgency and motivating users to give before the deadline.

#### Flux Metrics Affected

The Flux Metrics analyze the three primary metrics that affect revenue (traffic, conversion rate, and average gift). This experiment produced the following results:

52.7% increase in traffic  
× 0% increase in conversion rate  
× 20.4% increase in average gift  
84.0% increase in revenue

#### Key Learnings

The key learning from this experiment is that adding a countdown clock to the website's lower-third banner significantly increased engagement with the call to action. Visitors who saw the countdown clock clicked the CTA button **52.7% more often** than those in the control group, reaching a **98.5% level of statistical confidence**.

We also measured downstream donations and revenue from those clicks. While the treatment drove more visitors to the donation page, it did **not** produce a corresponding increase in completed gifts or revenue. This suggests the countdown clock was effective at encouraging users to take the next step but was not enough on its own to increase donor conversion.

Even so, the results reinforce the value of urgency as a tool for driving engagement. Future experiments should continue exploring how urgency can be leveraged more effectively throughout the donor journey. This could include testing different countdown designs, stronger deadline messaging, or placing urgency cues closer to or directly on the donation page where the giving decision is made.

Overall, this experiment demonstrates that real-time urgency can be an effective way to increase interaction with campaign assets. The next opportunity is to pair that increased engagement with stronger donation page messaging and optimization to convert more of those additional visitors into donors.
