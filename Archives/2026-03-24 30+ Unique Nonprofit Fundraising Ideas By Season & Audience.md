## Community Events & Activities

Unique fundraising ideas that bring people together in person can generate both funds and goodwill. These community-focused events—from raffles to concerts to food festivals—offer engaging ways to support your cause. *Works best for: service clubs, local nonprofits, and any organization with a venue and a volunteer team to run the event.*

### Traditional Raffle Fundraising

Classic raffles remain one of the most effective fundraising methods when executed well. These events use [custom raffle tickets](https://products.eventgroove.com/raffle-tickets/) sold to participants for a chance to win prizes. The key is selecting prizes that excite your audience and pricing tickets strategically for maximum participation. Discover [great raffle prize ideas](https://www.eventgroove.com/blog/great-raffle-prize-ideas/) that drive ticket sales.

### Tricky Tray Fundraisers

Also known as basket raffles, tricky tray events let participants bid on multiple prize baskets using packets of specialized [tricky tray tickets](https://products.eventgroove.com/ticket-sheets/tags/tricky-tray-tickets/). This format creates more excitement than traditional single-prize raffles and often generates higher revenue per participant. Learn [what tricky tray fundraising is](https://products.eventgroove.com/blog/articles/whats-a-tricky-tray-fundraiser-and-can-it-work-for-you/) and how it can work for your organization.

### 50/50 Raffle Fundraising

These popular events split the total pot between the winner and your organization, making them easy to promote since prize value grows with participation. No need to source prizes – the fundraising itself creates the reward. Learn [how](https://products.eventgroove.com/blog/articles/50-50-raffle-fundraiser-explained/) [50/50 raffles work](https://products.eventgroove.com/blog/articles/50-50-raffle-fundraiser-explained/) and why they’re so effective.

### Charity Concerts

Music brings people together and creates memorable fundraising experiences that can generate significant support while showcasing local talent. These events require careful coordination but offer high impact potential. Follow our [charity concert planning guide](https://www.eventgroove.com/blog/how-to-plan-a-great-charity-concert-in-6-steps/) for successful execution.

### Food Fundraising Events

Community food events like BBQ fundraisers, pancake breakfasts, and dinner nights are time-tested fundraising strategies that bring people together around meals. These events work especially well for service clubs, sports teams, and community organizations. QR codes can modernize these traditional events by streamlining ordering and payments. Learn [how to enhance food fundraisers](https://www.eventgroove.com/blog/how-to-use-qr-codes-to-cook-up-a-great-fundraiser-2/) with QR codes.

## Quick Start Guide: Choosing Your Fundraising Idea

Not sure where to begin? These quick tips can help you choose easy fundraising ideas that match your timeline, audience, and budget.

### Match Your Audience

- **Faith communities**: Easter events, community service challenges
- **Sports organizations**: A-thon events, team-based peer-to-peer campaigns, football fundraising
- **Health nonprofits**: Awareness month campaigns, education-focused events
- **Emergency services**: Community festivals, appreciation events
- **Corporate teams**: Virtual CSR campaigns, employee engagement events
- **Schools & youth groups**: Sports fundraising, seasonal events, peer-to-peer challenges
