Google Ads is evolving — again. This time, the change is not just a small tweak to bidding strategies, or a new ad extension. It’s something much bigger: AI Max for Search.

Launched in May 2025, AI Max is Google’s newest suite of AI-powered features designed to make Search campaigns more effective. It combines intelligent keyword matching, automated creative generation, and optimized landing page selection — all in one toggle. According to Google, [advertisers who activate AI Max see an average 14% lift in conversions or conversion value at a similar cost per acquisition](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-ai-max-for-search-campaigns/#introduction) (CPA) or return on ad spend (ROAS).

But what exactly is AI Max for Search advertising? How does it work, and how can you use it to your advantage? Let’s break it down.

## What is AI Max for Search?

AI Max for Search is a set of AI-powered enhancements for Google Ads Search campaigns, including within Search campaigns for [Ad Grant](https://wholewhale.com/resources/google-maps-placements-for-ad-grants/) users. It’s not a new campaign type, but rather an optional campaign-wide optimization strategy you can turn on to unlock advanced automation.

Once activated, AI Max optimizes three main areas:

### 1\. Search term matching

- AI Max reaches more relevant queries through AI-driven broad match and [keywordless targeting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15910187?hl=en) to find relevant queries you might not be targeting manually.
- Pulls context from your keywords, ad creatives, and landing page content to match with the right searches.
- Helps capture new, high-intent traffic that traditional keyword targeting might miss.

**For example:** A nonprofit animal shelter may have a specific goal to increase local dog adoption. After enabling AI Max search term matching, Google AI learns from their existing ad copy (i.e. “Adopt a Dog” “Foster a Puppy”), and landing pages (“Help stray dogs,” “Sponsor a dog”). It begins matching to relevant, long-tail, and previously uncovered queries such as: foster a senior dog near me”, “volunteer dog walking \[city\]”, “adopt pit bull rescue”.

### 2\. Text customization

- AI Max automatically generates headlines and descriptions using your existing assets, landing page copy, and relevant keyword themes.
- Tests variations in real-time to improve CTR (click-through rate) and Quality Score.
- Works similarly to responsive search ads, but with more AI-driven variation and adaptation.

For example: Instead of manually writing 15 headlines, AI Max can take the animal shelter’s existing ad copy and landing page copy and create additional headline variations like “Adopt a Rescue Dog” or “Meet Your Furry Match Today” tailored to different search queries.

### 3\. Final URL expansion

- AI Max can dynamically send users to the most relevant page on your site, not just the one you manually set.
- Helps improves engagement rates by sending users directly to the content most relevant to their search interests
- Requires text customization to be enabled, ensuring the ad copy aligns with the chosen landing page.

For example: When someone searches “adopt a rescue dog,” AI Max can send them directly to the animal shelter’s “Adopt Dogs” page instead of to the shelter homepage, reducing clicks to action and improving engagement rates.

## AI Max: AI-Assisted, Expert-Led Advertising

What makes AI Max especially appealing is the balance between automation and control. Unlike [Performance Max](https://wholewhale.com/resources/google-maps-placements-for-ad-grants/), which limits advertiser control in many ways, AI Max allows you to use brand controls, location targeting refinements, and URL inclusion/exclusion settings alongside automated features.

In short: AI Max is Google’s way of combining the precision of Search with the scale and intelligence of AI — without forcing you into a “black box.”

**AI Max is powerful, but you don’t have to give up all control. Google provides:**

- **Location of Interest targeting:** In standard Search campaigns, location targeting is set at the campaign level. With AI Max, you get an additional control at the ad group level called “locations of interest.” This lets you prioritize customers who show intent toward a place
- **Brand controls:** Choose which brands your ads can be associated with, and which to exclude. Using the same Dog nonprofit example:
	- Include partner brands you’re comfortable being associated with (i.e. a local shelter partner, Pet supplies stores).
		- Exclude brands that signal misaligned intent. (i.e. breeder/retail brands for puppies.
- **URL inclusion/exclusion**: Decide which pages AI Max can or cannot send traffic to.

## How Nonprofits Can Get the Most Out of AI Max

### 1\. Start Small

Test AI Max on one or two Google Ads or Ad Grant search campaigns first. Measure the impact before rolling it out to your entire account.

### 2\. Optimize Your Copy

Because AI Max relies on your landing page content to understand relevance and automate assets, optimize your key pages to include clear, substantial, and well-structured copy. Aim for enough depth to fully answer a visitor’s search intent while still making it easy to scan. Include the language your audience uses, covering primary synonyms and related topics.

### 3\. Use Controls Wisely

If you’re nervous about losing control, start by enabling only Text Customization or Search Term Matching. Add Final URL Expansion later. Regular audits and revisions will further help in fine-tuning the control settings, i.e. including exclusions around sensitive topics. Ensuring that the AI remains aligned with the overall marketing strategy while still benefiting from automated optimizations.

### 4\. Monitor the “AI Max” Match Type

It’s important to monitor how conversion rates and costs for AI Max campaigns compare to standard campaigns — review results regularly, and optimize budget allocation accordingly across AI Max vs. traditional campaign types.

### 5\. Audit Your Site Language

AI Max can generate headlines from *any* content on your site when final URL expansion is enabled. Review your pages to ensure all site messaging is brand-safe and conversion-friendly.

### 6\. Pair with Smart Bidding

AI Max works well with automated bidding strategies like Target CPA or Target ROAS, leveraging the AI synergy for better results.
