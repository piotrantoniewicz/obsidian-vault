Have you ever checked out ReallyGoodEmails.com?

It's a platform that showcases thousands of marketing emails - *really good ones 😉* - from some of your favorite brands.

You can look through different categories and industries to get inspired and see what innovative marketing ideas other businesses are up to.

It's pretty fun!

However, if you search “nonprofit” or “fundraising,” not much comes up that's actually helpful…💔👎.

The few emails that do pop up are:

❌ Filled with lots of images

❌ Long paragraphs and sentences

❌ Not personal

Everything I preach *against*!

Once again, as a fundraiser, I was feeling out in the boonies.

Wouldn't it be so cool to see *really good nonprofit emails* out in the wild? Emails that you know converted and had positive results?

I think so too.
