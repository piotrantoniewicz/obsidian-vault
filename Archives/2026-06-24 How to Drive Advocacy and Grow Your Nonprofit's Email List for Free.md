For decades, nonprofits have joined forces around shared causes. Whether advocating for policy change, mobilizing supporters around urgent issues, or raising public awareness, coalition campaigns have long been one of the sector’s most powerful tools.

But traditional joint actions often came with tradeoffs. Supporters could end up hearing from organizations they didn’t expect. Participating organizations sometimes struggled to see meaningful value beyond supporting the cause. Campaign hosts often had to balance coalition-building with supporter experience.

[**Civic Shout’s Joint Actions platform**](https://civicshout.com/joint-actions) was built to solve these challenges. By making coalition campaigns privacy-friendly and consent-forward, Civic Shout enables organizations to work together while creating value for everyone involved: supporters, participating organizations, and campaign hosts.

Instead of automatically sharing supporter data across an entire coalition, Civic Shout Joint Actions allow supporters to decide exactly which organizations they want to hear from. Participating organizations can earn ad credits that help them grow and engage their own audiences. Hosts benefit from all of those same advantages, plus everyone who signs the petition can join the host’s email list.

The result is a modern approach to collaborative advocacy that is helping nonprofits grow their impact in five powerful ways.

## 1) Growing Email Lists at No Cost

Email remains one of the most valuable assets a nonprofit can build. It drives advocacy, fundraising, volunteer recruitment, and long-term supporter engagement.

Through Civic Shout Joint Actions, organizations can either **[host joint actions](https://civicshout.com/joint-actions/host)** or **[participate in joint actions hosted by others](https://civicshout.com/joint-actions/participate)**. Participating organizations earn a $2.25 ad credit for every petition signature they generate. Each $2.25 credit can be redeemed for three nationally targeted clicks to their own petition or letter campaign.

That means if an organization generates 1,000 signatures for a joint action, it earns $2,250 in Civic Shout credits, good for approximately 3,000 nationally targeted clicks to one of its own campaigns.

Hosts receive that same participant benefit, plus everyone who signs the petition joins the host’s list. In other words, participating organizations can grow their lists at no cost through earned credits, while hosts can grow directly from the campaign itself.

Dream.org’s Gabby Vice-Lamonica said Civic Shout Joint Actions have “helped us easily grow our list and helped others take action,” capturing the collaborative nature of the model.

Even organizations already investing in audience growth have found meaningful value. Common Cause’s Devon Bhakta said, “I was pleasantly surprised by the number of NTL we received, considering we already do a lot of paid acquisition with Civic Shout.”

For many nonprofits, the appeal is simple: Joint Actions make it possible to help another organization advance an important campaign while earning tangible value for your own work.

## 2) Reactivating Supporters Who Have Gone Quiet

Most nonprofits have supporters who care deeply about their mission but have become less engaged over time.

Perhaps they signed a petition years ago, donated during a major campaign, or joined an email list but haven’t taken action recently. Re-engaging those supporters can be difficult.

Joint Actions provide a powerful way to bring those supporters back into the fold.

Because Joint Actions focus on timely, high-interest issues, they create compelling opportunities for supporters to take action again. Organizations can also use the credits they earn through participation to drive supporters into their own petitions, campaigns, and advocacy programs.

One participating organization generated 10,000 signatures for a Joint Action campaign, earning $22,500 in Civic Shout credits. The organization later redeemed those credits to drive approximately 30,000 clicks to its own campaigns, resulting in more than 18,000 reactivated subscribers.

Rather than allowing supporters to remain dormant, Joint Actions can help organizations reconnect people with causes they already care about.

## 3) Amplifying Advocacy Through Collective Action

At its core, advocacy is about building enough public pressure to create change.

When multiple organizations care about the same issue, working together often creates far greater impact than working separately.

[**Civic Shout Joint Actions**](https://civicshout.com/joint-actions) allow organizations to unite around shared campaigns, combining their reach and mobilizing supporters toward a common goal. Instead of dozens of organizations running separate actions on the same issue, they can collaborate around a single campaign that generates more signatures, greater visibility, and stronger public pressure.

Importantly, this collaboration doesn’t come at the expense of supporter trust. Supporters choose exactly which organizations they want to hear from, creating a more transparent and respectful experience than traditional coalition models. Organizations benefit from higher-quality relationships, and supporters maintain control over their inboxes and personal information.

For supporters, participating organizations, and campaign hosts alike, that’s a win-win-win.

## 4) Creating New Fundraising Opportunities

Advocacy and fundraising are often viewed as separate activities, but many nonprofits know they are deeply connected.

Today’s petition signer can become tomorrow’s volunteer, recurring donor, event attendee, or major supporter. But Civic Shout Joint Actions can also help generate fundraising results much sooner.

When organizations redeem their Joint Action credits to promote petitions or letter campaigns on their own websites, they can pair those campaigns with immediate post-action donation asks. That means credits earned from supporting another organization’s campaign can later drive traffic to a campaign that grows the organization’s own list and generates donations.

Some organizations are already generating thousands of dollars in immediate post-action donations this way.

The pathway is straightforward: signatures generate credits, credits generate clicks, clicks generate supporters, and supporters can generate donations.

The downstream donor performance has been especially strong. Jacob Loyd of Animal Welfare Investigations Project said, “The names we get from Civic Shout convert to donors at the highest rate I’ve ever seen!”

By helping organizations acquire engaged activists through transparent, consent-based participation, Joint Actions can become both the first step in a long-term supporter journey and a near-term fundraising opportunity.
