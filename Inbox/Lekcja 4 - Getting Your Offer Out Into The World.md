Holy cow, you have made it to day four of the five-day zero to 250 list to launch challenge.
Congratulations! You've made it farther than most and I simply want for you to pat yourself on the
back just for showing up today. Now today I'm going to teach you how to get your offer out
into the world. So yesterday you learned how to create a sign-up form and a delivery email
and now today we're going to focus on getting it out into the world. Now that you have a place for
people to sign up and join your email list to get their hands on your offer, let's share it with
everyone. Now trust me, I get it. This part is likely going to feel the most uncomfortable.
You have to put yourself out there which is totally awkward, am I right? But here's the thing,
in order for you to grow, you have to be willing to become your own publicist. You have to believe
in what you're doing so much that it's a disservice if you don't go out and share it. So let's just
start simple and let's start with who's already there. Chances are, regardless of where you're
at in your journey, you probably already have a Facebook account with friends and family
and an Instagram account with followers. And yes, even 10 followers, it totally counts.
So I don't want for you to trick yourself into believing that you have to grow your social media
first before you can start your email list. No, that is a lie. Have you ever heard somebody say,
I need to get in shape first before I attend that exercise class? And you're like, wait a second,

have to do that work first. You can do them together. You can do them in tandem and you can
start with what you've got. So here are five tips for sharing your offer with the world in a non-spammy
