## Step-by-Step Guide to Creating a Free Fundraising Site

### 1\. Define Your Fundraising Goal

Defining your fundraising goal is like setting the GPS for your campaign—it tells everyone where you’re headed and gives you a clear direction. Think about what you’re raising money for, whether it’s a specific project, covering operational costs, or funding an upcoming event. Be specific!

For example, instead of saying, “We need money for supplies,” try, “We’re raising $10,000 to provide 500 backpacks filled with school supplies for local kids.” A clear, tangible goal that you can divide into [donation tiers](https://www.causevox.com/blog/donation-tiers/) helps your supporters understand the impact of their contribution and gets them excited to help you reach that finish line!

## 3\. Choose Your Platform

Choosing the right platform for your fundraising site is key to making your campaign a success. Look for one that aligns with your needs, is easy to use, and provides a great experience for both you and your donors. The right platform should feel like a partner in your efforts.

### Compare Features and Limitations

Take a close look at what [each platform](https://www.causevox.com/blog/fundraising-platforms-for-nonprofits/) offers. Are there transaction fees, and how much are they? Is there an option for your donors to cover the fees? Does it let you customize your page to [reflect your brand](https://www.causevox.com/blog/stand-out-nonprofit-branding/)? Is it simple for both you and your donors to navigate? Weighing these details can help you make the best choice for your campaign.

## 4\. Create Your Fundraising Site

Once you’ve chosen your platform, it’s time to create your fundraising site, which is all about making a great first impression! Start by branding your page—make sure your colors, logo, and messaging feel cohesive and true to your nonprofit. Then craft a clear, engaging headline that grabs attention. Use visuals like photos or videos to make it personal and relatable—they help donors connect with your cause.

![fundraising-thermometer](https://www.causevox.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/no-shave-fundraising-thermometer-2048x1011.png)

*Using a fundraising progress meter excites people to help you reach and raise even more than your goal.*

Be sure to highlight your fundraising goal and use a progress meter or [fundraising thermometer](https://www.causevox.com/fundraising-thermometer/) to encourage donors to help you reach the goal! Pair this with a strong, visible call-to-action like “Donate Now” or “Make an Impact Today” to guide supporters to take the next step. Keep it bold, easy to find, and impossible to miss!

## 5\. Craft an Engaging Fundraising Story

When donors scroll down your campaign page, they should see a heartfelt story. Crafting an engaging [fundraising story](https://www.causevox.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-storytelling-for-nonprofits/) is all about making people feel something. Share the “why” behind your cause—what inspired this campaign and who will it help? Introduce real people, situations, or moments connected to your mission to make it personal.

Be honest and heartfelt, and don’t shy away from showing the challenges you’re working to overcome. People want to know their support makes a difference, so paint a clear picture of the impact they can have. Whether it’s through words, photos, or videos, your story should inspire donors to feel like they’re part of something meaningful and urgent.

## Promoting Your Fundraising Site

Promoting your fundraising site is all about getting the word out to as many people as possible. Share it on your [social media platforms](https://www.causevox.com/blog/effective-social-media-planning-for-nonprofit-fundraising-success/) –like Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn–to tap into your followers and their networks. Don’t forget your existing donors’ [email newsletters](https://www.causevox.com/blog/nonprofit-email-marketing-guide/) are a great way to keep them informed and encourage them to support your cause again. You can also collaborate with influencers or [partners](https://www.causevox.com/blog/nonprofit-partnerships/) to amplify your reach and introduce your campaign to a whole new audience. Finally, don’t forget to update your page regularly to show progress and keep your supporters in the loop. When people see how their donations are making a difference, they’re more likely to stay involved!
