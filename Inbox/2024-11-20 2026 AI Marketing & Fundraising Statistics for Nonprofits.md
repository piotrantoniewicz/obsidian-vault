## AI in the Nonprofit Sector

- 70% of nonprofits believe AI can help reduce workload and improve communications, but 60% say they lack the in-house expertise to assess tools, and only 4% have AI-specific training budgets. \[[AI Equity Project](https://aiequityproject.my.canva.site/2025)\]
- 70% of nonprofit professionals are concerned about data privacy and security, 63% worry about accuracy, and 57% are concerned about representation and biases in the use of generative AI. \[[Nonprofit Perspectives on Generative AI Report](https://www.jrf.org.uk/ai-for-public-good/grassroots-and-non-profit-perspectives-on-generative-ai)\]
- 86% of nonprofits do not have an AI policy. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Of the nonprofits that use AI, 38% report a positive impact while 13% report a negative impact. \[[AI for Good Repor](https://ai-adoption.3sidedcube.com/) t\]
- More than a third of nonprofit professionals (37%) are actively involved in wider discussions on AI. Those not involved cite a lack of awareness of opportunities for involvement, not being asked to get involved, and resource limitations. \[[Nonprofit Perspectives on Generative AI Report](https://www.jrf.org.uk/ai-for-public-good/grassroots-and-non-profit-perspectives-on-generative-ai)\]
- Nonprofit board members say the top three benefits of using AI are: minutes automation (82%), document summaries (75%), and director prep (63%). \[[AI in the Nonprofit Boardroom Survey](https://www.onboardmeetings.com/resources/ai-in-the-nonprofit-boardroom/?utm_source=other&utm_campaign=nt4g_ai_survey&utm_medium=edit&utm_content=np_tech_for_good)\]

## AI in Nonprofit Marketing

- The top three reasons nonprofits use AI are for internal productivity (35%), marketing and communications (31%), and development and fundraising (24%). \[[The Center for Effective Philanthropy](https://cep.org/report-backpacks/state-of-nonprofits-2024-what-funders-need-to-know/?section=intro)\]
- 51% of nonprofits use AI chatbots for marketing and communications. Of those, 75% use ChatGPT, 20% use Microsoft Copilot, 13% use Claude, and 13% use Gemini. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- The top three ways nonprofits use AI chatbots are to check grammar, spelling, and punctuation (53%), to help brainstorm headlines and subject lines (53%), and to create first drafts of content (39%). \[[Nonprofit Communications Trends Report](https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/the-nonprofit-communications-trends-report/)\]
- 82% of nonprofits are using AI chatbots informally/ad-hoc, mostly to generate content like drafting donor emails. \[[AI Equity Project](https://mailchi.mp/namastedata/ai-equity)\]
- Only 17% of nonprofits pay a monthly subscription for an AI chatbot. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- Of the nonprofits that say they use AI, 42% say they use it for data analysis and 24% for internal automation. \[[2026 Nonprofit AI Adoption Report](https://539405.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/539405/AI%20Report/Virtuous%202026%20Nonprofit%20AI%20Adoption%20Report.pdf)\]
- 20% of nonprofits use an AI tool to create and publish images/graphics and 1% use an AI tool to create and publish video content. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- 69% of nonprofit marketers that use generative AI have not received formal training. \[[Nonprofit Perspectives on Generative AI Report](https://www.jrf.org.uk/ai-for-public-good/grassroots-and-non-profit-perspectives-on-generative-ai)\]

## AI in Nonprofit Fundraising

- 9% of nonprofits utilize smart fundraising donation forms and 3% use real-time fundraising intelligence. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- The average one-time donation for donation forms utilizing AI without using personally identifiable information (PII) is $161 compared to the industry average of $115, and the average monthly recurring donation is $32 compared to the industry average of $24. \[[Fundraise Up](https://fundraiseup.com/blog/predictive-ai-for-nonprofits/)\]
- 13% of nonprofits use predictive AI software for donor prospecting. \[[State of AI in Nonprofits](https://page.techsoup.org/ai-benchmark-report-2025?utm_campaign=6825364-2025%20AI%20Benchmark%20Report&utm_source=pressrelease)\]
- Only 2% of nonprofits utilize agentic AI fundraising software. \[[2026 Nonprofit Tech for Good Report](https://report.nptechforgood.com/2026)\]
- 30% of nonprofits say they are interested in using fully autonomous AI fundraising software, but need more peer proof and case studies. \[[2026 State of Autonomous Fundraising Report](https://givzey.com/state-of-autonomous-fundraising-2026)\]
- 4.5% of donors use chatbots, like ChatGPT and Claude, to find and research causes. \[[Brand Discovery in the age of AI Report](https://www.bluestate.co/landingpage/brand-discovery-in-the-age-of-ai/)\]
- 63% of fundraisers are unsure about using generative AI for donor communications because it seems less personal, but 82% are comfortable using AI for donor research. \[[The Nonprofit Productivity Report\]](https://www.givemomentum.com/resources/nonprofit-productivity-report)
- 76% of donors say it’s important for nonprofits to clearly disclose when and how AI is used, yet only 26% of nonprofit professionals say their organization does this today. \[[2026 Bridging the AI Effectiveness Gap](https://institute.blackbaud.com/resources/ai-effectiveness-gap?utm_medium=paid+email&utm_source=nptechforgood&utm_campaign=BBI-US-26-RP-StateofAISocialImpactReport&utm_content=nptechforgood)\]
- The more generous the donor, the more likely they are to support nonprofits using AI – 30% for high donors, 19% for medium donors, and 13% for small donors. \[[Charities Aid Foundation](https://www.cafonline.org/insights/research/what-the-public-think-of-charities-using-ai)\]
- Donors who give as a result of visiting a nonprofit’s website from a chatbot are less likely to donate right away, but when they do give, the average gift is $250. \[[Brand Discovery in the age of AI Report](https://www.bluestate.co/landingpage/brand-discovery-in-the-age-of-ai/)\]
- 23% of foundations will not accept grant applications with content created by generative AI. 10% will. 67% are undecided about whether to accept AI-generated applications. \[[Candid.org](https://blog.candid.org/post/funders-insights-on-ai-generated-grant-application-proposals/)\]
